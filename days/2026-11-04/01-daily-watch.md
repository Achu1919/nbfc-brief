## 🔴 URGENT / High Impact

### Muthoot Finance (peer) discloses GST search at its registered office
<!-- tags: companies=muthoot-finance; topics=gold-loan,compliance; category=urgent -->
Muthoot Finance Ltd, a separate listed company, told the exchanges late on 2 Nov that the GST Department ran a search under Section 67 of the CGST Act at its registered office from 29 Oct (about 11:00 IST) to 30 Oct (about 21:30 IST). The company said operations were unaffected and that it received the search mahasar on 2 Nov.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: none directly, as the search concerns a different company; the disclosure coincides with the final days of Muthoot Fincorp's Tranche I NCD issue.
Source: [NSE filing (Muthoot Finance)](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/MUTHOOTFIN_02112026202440_Reg_30_GST_SearchSD.pdf)

### Business Today reads the Muthoot Fincorp DRHP: gold first, then wider secured lending
<!-- tags: companies=muthoot-fincorp; topics=ipo,gold-loan,housing-finance; category=muthoot-fincorp -->
Business Today's 2 Nov review of the DRHP for the ₹3,000 crore fresh-issue IPO notes gold-loan AUM of ₹49,331.20 crore at 31 March 2026 (59.04% CAGR over two years) across 3,781 gold-loan branches. The company plans to grow loans against property, business loans and housing loans, and fee income rose from ₹211.59 crore in FY24 to ₹571.76 crore in FY26.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: the press is framing the IPO as diversification beyond gold, so Q2 disclosures on the non-gold book are likely to be read against it.
Source: [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/markets/story/muthoot-fincorp-looks-beyond-gold-loans-with-rs3000-crore-ipo-565234-2026-11-02)

### RBI amends FEMA export-import regulations
<!-- tags: regulators=rbi; topics=regulation; category=regulation -->
RBI published Notification No. FEMA 23(R)/(2)/2026-RB dated 2 Nov 2026, the Second Amendment to the Export and Import of Goods and Services Regulations, in force from 1 Oct 2026. It covers foreign-exchange rules for exporters and importers, not NBFC lending.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: none for lending.

## 💰 Financial Results & Performance

### Northern Arc Q2 PAT up 41% to ₹129 crore; lending AUM ₹19,003 crore
<!-- tags: companies=northern-arc; topics=results; category=results -->
Northern Arc Capital's Q2 FY27 PAT rose 41% year on year to ₹129 crore and NII rose 32% to ₹425 crore. Lending AUM grew 34% to ₹19,003 crore, with credit cost at 2.55% and RoA at 2.82%.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: a diversified retail and MSME lender growing AUM at 34% with lower credit costs is a benchmark for non-gold growth at gold lenders.
Source: [NSE filing (Northern Arc)](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/NACL2020_03112026173910_Press_release_Q2_Sep2026_SD.pdf)

### Fusion Finance returns to profit at ₹86.5 crore; GNPA 1.6%
<!-- tags: companies=fusion-finance; topics=results,microfinance; category=results -->
Microfinance lender Fusion Finance earned ₹86.5 crore in Q2, against ₹62.4 crore in Q1 and a ₹22.1 crore loss a year earlier. GNPA fell to 1.6%, AUM grew 14% to ₹8,007 crore and NIM widened to 12.5%.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: the microfinance recovery is showing in lender P&Ls ahead of Muthoot Microfin's 5 Nov results.

## 🏘️ Sector Deep Dive — Wednesday: Housing finance

### LIC Housing Finance targets GNPA of 1.9% after ₹553 crore of Q2 recoveries
<!-- tags: companies=lic-housing-finance; topics=housing-finance,asset-quality; category=housing-finance -->
LIC Housing Finance recovered ₹553 crore from its retail book in Q2 and ₹84 crore from written-off accounts, and GNPA was 2.05% at 30 September (2.51% a year earlier). MD Sandeep Kumar targets 1.9% by March and 14–15% disbursement growth, to ₹73,000–75,000 crore in FY27.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: the largest HFC is chasing growth through retention and project loans, keeping prime home-loan pricing competitive for lenders growing housing books.
Source: [Mint](https://www.livemint.com/industry/banking/lic-housing-finance-assets-loans-companies-retail-portfolio-disbursements-11793676974950.html)

### HFC margins under pressure: LIC HFL raises its best rate 25 bps to 7.40%
<!-- tags: companies=lic-housing-finance; topics=housing-finance,funding; category=housing-finance -->
LIC HFL's incremental borrowing cost is 6.91% against a cumulative 7.29%, and NIM was 2.58% in Q2 against 2.62% a year earlier. It raised lending rates 25 bps, taking the best prime home-loan rate from 7.15% to 7.40%, and keeps NIM guidance at 2.5–2.6%.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: rate hikes are now reaching prime home-loan rates, which gives smaller lenders room to reprice LAP and home loans.
Source: [Mint](https://www.livemint.com/industry/banking/lic-housing-finance-assets-loans-companies-retail-portfolio-disbursements-11793676974950.html)

### ED registers PMLA case against Subhash Chandra on LIC HFL complaint
<!-- tags: companies=lic-housing-finance; topics=housing-finance,fraud; category=housing-finance -->
The Enforcement Directorate registered a PMLA case against Essel Group founder Subhash Chandra on 2 Nov, based on a CBI FIR arising from LIC HFL's complaint about two 2018 loans of ₹980 crore that turned non-performing. LIC HFL says it holds equivalent security; its admitted claim in Chandra's personal insolvency is ₹1,322.39 crore.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: the case highlights reliance on guarantor net-worth certificates in large secured loans, relevant to any lender's LAP and business-loan underwriting.
Source: [Mint](https://www.livemint.com/industry/banking/lic-housing-finance-assets-loans-companies-retail-portfolio-disbursements-11793676974950.html)

### HFC stocks: LIC Housing +1.95%, PNB Housing +0.25%, Bajaj Housing flat
<!-- tags: companies=lic-housing-finance,pnb-housing,bajaj-housing-finance; topics=housing-finance,markets; category=housing-finance -->
On 3 Nov LIC Housing Finance closed at ₹504.95 (+1.95%), PNB Housing Finance at ₹1,063.00 (+0.25%) and Bajaj Housing Finance at ₹78.19 (+0.01%). HFCs lagged the 2.15% rise in the Nifty Financial Services Ex-Bank index.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: markets are pricing slower margins for housing lenders than for diversified NBFCs.
Source: [NSE bhavcopy](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/products/content/sec_bhavdata_full_03112026.csv)

> **Read for Muthoot Fincorp:** Muthoot Fincorp's DRHP lists housing loans and LAP among the secured lines it plans to grow. Today's HFC data show rising borrowing costs, a 25 bp rate hike by the largest HFC to protect NIM, and recoveries rather than growth carrying asset quality.
