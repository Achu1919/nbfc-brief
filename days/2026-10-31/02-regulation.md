## 🔔 New Regulatory Updates Today

One new RBI circular names NBFCs, and it's routine. **RBI/2026-27/303** (DoR.AML.REC.255/14.06.001/2026-27, 30 Oct) passes on the UNSC's amendments to five entries on the **1988 (2011) Taliban Sanctions List** under Section 51A of UAPA. It's addressed to banks, **NBFCs**, ARCs and AIFIs and points back to Chapter IX of the KYC Directions, 2025. ([RBI](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=13749&Mode=0))
- **[NEW] [EFFECTIVE:2026-10-30]**
