## 🔴 URGENT / High Impact

### Manappuram's Q2: gold AUM up 6.4% in the quarter, profit ₹701 crore
<!-- story: id=manappuram-q2fy27-results; milestone=results -->
<!-- tags: companies=manappuram; topics=results,gold-loan,asset-quality; category=results -->
Manappuram Finance reported consolidated Q2 FY27 profit of ₹701 crore, up 19.9% on the quarter and 222.9% on the year, with consolidated gold-loan AUM of ₹60,640 crore, up 6.4% QoQ and 92.5% YoY. Standalone gross Stage 3 was 1.80% at 30 September, and the standalone LTV slipped to 64.5% from 65.6% in June.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: this is the first full set of gold-lender numbers since gold began falling this week, and other gold lenders' Q2 numbers, including Muthoot Fincorp's, will be read against it. Manappuram's gold book grew faster than its tonnage (6.4% against 3.5%), so a good part of the growth came from higher gold prices and bigger tickets, which is the part a falling gold price reverses.
Sources: [NSE: results](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/MANAPPURAM_12112026164223_MAFILOUTCOMEFINAL.pdf) · [NSE: investor deck](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/MANAPPURAM_12112026164750_IPmerged.pdf) · [NSE: press release](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/MANAPPURAM_12112026195139_PressReleaseFinal.pdf) · [CNBC-TV18](https://www.cnbctv18.com/market/earnings/manappuram-finance-q2-net-profit-more-than-doubles-to-rs701-crore-nii-rises-45pc-20032930.htm)

### Gold hits a 13-month low and AGLOC cuts the 85% ceiling by ₹227 a gram
<!-- story: id=gold-price-weekly -->
<!-- tags: topics=gold-price,gold-loan,macro; category=markets -->
AGLOC's 85% LTV ceiling for 13 Nov is ₹10,720 a gram, down from ₹10,947 on 12 Nov, on a jewellery reference price of ₹12,612 (was ₹12,879). IBJA's 12 Nov PM rate for 999 gold was ₹1,37,692 per 10 g, and Reuters had spot gold down 2.7% at $3,880.93 an ounce on Thursday afternoon in New York, its lowest since October 2025, as oil rose and traders priced an 87% chance of a December Fed hike.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: the industry's 85% per-gram ceiling on fresh gold loans drops by ₹227 from today, the biggest single-day cut this month, and the ceiling is now ₹293 below the 7–11 Nov level. Loans written at last week's ceiling now sit close to their cap.
Sources: [AGLOC](https://www.agloc.org/) · [IBJA](https://ibjarates.com/) · [Mint (Reuters)](https://www.livemint.com/market/gold-drops-to-over-one-year-low-as-fading-middle-east-peace-hopes-fan-inflation-fears-11794511222562.html) · [ET Now](https://www.etnownews.com/markets/gold-price-today-november-13-gold-trades-below-3900-as-us-dollar-hits-52-week-high-fed-rate-hike-bets-remain-firm-article-156501212)

### RBI is drafting curbs on private equity control of more than one NBFC, Reuters reports
<!-- story: id=rbi-pe-nbfc-control-curbs-2026; continues=nbfc-foreign-investment-2026 -->
<!-- tags: companies=manappuram,iifl-finance; regulators=rbi; topics=policy,funding,m-and-a; category=regulation -->
RBI is working on proposals to stop a private equity firm holding a controlling stake in more than one NBFC, two people told Reuters; holdings of 15% or less would be treated as financial investments with lighter scrutiny. A draft would go out for public consultation, and the rules could be issued early next year, one source said; RBI did not comment.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: this is a press report, not an RBI instrument. If it goes through, PE money can still come in as minority stakes of 15% or less, which is the size most IPO anchor and pre-IPO investors take, but a fund that already controls another NBFC couldn't take control of a second one.
Sources: [Mint (Reuters)](https://www.livemint.com/industry/banking/india-plans-curbs-on-private-equity-ownership-of-nbfcs-report-11794472084478.html) · [Economic Times (Reuters)](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/economy/finance/rbi-plans-curbs-on-private-equity-firms-controlling-stakes-in-multiple-non-bank-lenders-report/articleshow/135763029.cms)

### Aditya Birla Capital opens its 100th gold-loan branch, targets 1,000+ in three years
<!-- story: id=aditya-birla-capital-gold-loan-2026 -->
<!-- tags: companies=aditya-birla-capital; topics=gold-loan; category=peers -->
Aditya Birla Capital opened its 100th dedicated gold-loan branch, in Santacruz, Mumbai, about three months after entering the business, and repeated its plan for 200–300 branches by March 2027 and more than 1,000 within three years. Its gold-loan AUM was ₹200 crore at 30 September.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: another well-funded group lender is building a branch-led gold franchise, starting in Maharashtra, which the release says produced 5.8% of India's gold-loan originations in 2025. It's small today, but it competes for the same branch staff and customers Muthoot Fincorp serves outside Kerala.
