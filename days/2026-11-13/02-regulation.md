## 🔔 New Regulatory Updates Today

No new RBI, SEBI, IBBI or NHB instrument for NBFCs on 12 Nov. Two things are still worth knowing: RBI refreshed its NBFC FAQ page, and Reuters reports RBI is drafting limits on private equity control of NBFCs.

### RBI refreshes its "All you wanted to know about NBFCs" FAQs
<!-- story: id=rbi-nbfc-faq-update-2026-11; single_source=official -->
<!-- tags: regulators=rbi; topics=policy; category=regulation -->
RBI's NBFC FAQ page now carries the line "Updated as on November 11, 2026". It covers registration under Section 45-IA, the ₹10 crore net owned fund floor (existing NBFCs have until 31 March 2027), scale-based layers, lending against shares at a 50% LTV, and the April 2026 Type I / Unregistered Type I NBFC framework.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: no new obligation for NBFCs. The FAQ restates existing rules; this edition did not compare it line by line with the previous version, so it reads as a refresh unless RBI flags a change.
Sources: [RBI FAQs](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/FAQView.aspx?Id=92)

### Watch item: RBI drafting curbs on private equity control of more than one NBFC (Reuters)
<!-- story: id=rbi-pe-nbfc-control-curbs-2026; dup -->
<!-- tags: regulators=rbi; topics=policy,funding; category=regulation -->
Two people told Reuters that RBI is working on proposals to stop a private equity firm holding a controlling stake in more than one NBFC, with holdings of 15% or less treated as financial investments. A draft would go to public consultation first. This is a press report, not an RBI instrument, and RBI did not comment.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: nothing changes until RBI publishes a draft. Under the existing Acquisition of Shareholding or Control Directions, 2025, any acquisition of control of an NBFC, or of 26% or more of its equity, already needs RBI's prior approval ([RBI Directions](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_ViewMasDirections.aspx?id=12964)).
Sources: [Mint (Reuters)](https://www.livemint.com/industry/banking/india-plans-curbs-on-private-equity-ownership-of-nbfcs-report-11794472084478.html) · [Economic Times (Reuters)](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/economy/finance/rbi-plans-curbs-on-private-equity-firms-controlling-stakes-in-multiple-non-bank-lenders-report/articleshow/135763029.cms)

SEBI's items were the Niveshak Shivir notice and appeal orders. NHB's latest posting is an 11 Nov procurement RFP, and the IBBI site was last updated 10 Nov. FIU-IND was not re-checked for this edition.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: no direct change. The dates still open are the 14 Nov Reg 52 results, the 21 Nov Credit Risk-o-Meter start and the 26 Nov PSL comment deadline.
