## 🔔 New Regulatory Updates Today

Two instruments landed on 11 Nov. Neither is addressed to NBFCs, but both touch KYC and tax-reporting processes that lenders also run.

### [NEW] RBI consolidates its Domestic Money Transfer Directions
<!-- reg: ref=RBI/DPSS/2026-27/476 CO.DPSS.POLC.No.S843/02-27-019/2026-2027; regulator=RBI; applies=banks,psos; issued=2026-11-11; effective=2026-11-11; status=new; lifecycle=final -->
<!-- tags: regulators=rbi; topics=circular,policy; category=circular -->
RBI issued the Reserve Bank of India (Domestic Money Transfer) Directions, 2026, which consolidate the existing DMT instructions for banks and authorised payment system operators and take effect immediately. Cash pay-outs stay capped at ₹10,000 a transaction and ₹25,000 a month per beneficiary, and walk-in remitters can send up to ₹5,000 a transaction and ₹25,000 a month through BCs, with registration on a verified mobile number and a self-certified OVD.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: not addressed to NBFCs; it applies to an NBFC only where it acts for a bank or payment operator. The velocity checks and alerts on unusual credit spurts in paras 15–17 describe controls also relevant to cash-heavy lending.
Reference: RBI/DPSS/2026-27/476 CO.DPSS.POLC.No.S843/02-27-019/2026-2027 · Addressed to: scheduled commercial banks (incl. RRBs), UCBs, StCBs, DCCBs, authorised payment system operators and participants · Issued: 11 Nov 2026 · Effective: 11 Nov 2026
Sources: [RBI notification](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=13752&Mode=0) · [RBI notifications feed](https://www.rbi.org.in/notifications_rss.xml)

### [NEW] SEBI aligns FATCA/CRS reporting with the Income-Tax Rules, 2026
<!-- reg: ref=HO/38/14/(18)2026-MIRSD-POD2/I/25560/2026; regulator=SEBI; applies=intermediaries,stock-exchanges,depositories; issued=2026-11-11; status=new; lifecycle=final -->
<!-- tags: regulators=sebi; topics=circular,policy; category=circular -->
SEBI told registered intermediaries that qualify as Reporting Financial Institutions to follow Rules 238 to 240 and Form 166 of the Income-Tax Rules, 2026, which replaced the 1962 Rules from 1 April 2026. They must update self-certification capture, controlling-person roles, account classification and Form 166 XML generation, and upload FATCA/CRS certifications to the KRAs.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: Muthoot Fincorp is not a SEBI intermediary. Reporting financial institutions outside SEBI's remit report under the same Income-Tax rules and Form 166, so the field changes SEBI lists apply to them as well.
Reference: HO/38/14/(18)2026-MIRSD-POD2/I/25560/2026 · Addressed to: all SEBI-registered intermediaries, stock exchanges and depositories · Issued: 11 Nov 2026
Sources: [SEBI circular](https://www.sebi.gov.in/legal/circulars/nov-2026/reporting-requirement-under-foreign-account-tax-compliance-act-fatca-and-common-reporting-standard-crs-revised-through-income-tax-act-2025-read-with-income-tax-rules-2026_105705.html) · [SEBI circular PDF](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/attachdocs/nov-2026/1794392501415.pdf)

### Watchlist sweep: nothing new for NBFCs on 11 Nov
RBI's other 11 Nov releases were money-market operations, T-bill auction results, a G-sec conversion and the money-supply data. SEBI's other items were recovery-proceeding notices and a settlement order. No new IBBI, NHB or FIU-IND instrument turned up in this run.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: no direct change. The dates still open are the 14 Nov Reg 52 results, the 21 Nov Credit Risk-o-Meter start and the 26 Nov PSL comment deadline.
Sources: [RBI press releases](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx) · [SEBI circulars](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebiweb/home/HomeAction.do?doListing=yes&sid=1&ssid=7&smid=0)
