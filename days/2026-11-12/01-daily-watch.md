# 📊 NBFC Daily Watch — Thursday, November 12, 2026

Morning edition for Thursday, 12 November 2026 (IST). Covers Wednesday 11 Nov and overnight. Stock and index closes are Wednesday's NSE files.

## 🔴 URGENT / High Impact

### Gold falls for a third session and AGLOC cuts its per-gram ceilings
<!-- story: id=gold-price-weekly -->
<!-- tags: topics=gold-price,gold-loan,macro; category=markets -->
AGLOC's 85% LTV ceiling for 12 Nov is ₹10,947 a gram, down from ₹11,013 for 7–11 Nov, on a jewellery reference price of ₹12,879 (was ₹12,957). IBJA's 11 Nov PM rate for 999 gold was ₹1,40,603 per 10 g, and Reuters had spot gold down 1.1% at $3,986.79 an ounce on Wednesday afternoon in New York after the hot US CPI print.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: the industry's 85% per-gram ceiling is ₹66 lower from 12 Nov, so gold lenders, Muthoot Fincorp among them, have less room per gram on fresh loans, and loans sanctioned at the earlier ceiling sit closer to their cap.
Sources: [AGLOC](https://www.agloc.org/) · [IBJA](https://ibjarates.com/) · [Reuters via New Straits Times](https://www.nst.com.my/business/economy/2026/11/1575844/gold-falls-hot-inflation-figures-higher-dollar-weigh) · [ET Now](https://www.etnownews.com/markets/gold-price-today-november-12-yellow-metal-slips-for-third-straight-session-falls-below-4000-as-fed-rate-hike-bets-rise-article-156496280)

### Bajaj Finance opens a QIP with a ₹992.25 floor price
<!-- story: id=bajaj-finance-capital-raise-2026; milestone=opened -->
<!-- tags: companies=bajaj-finance; topics=funding; category=deals -->
Bajaj Finance's QIP committee opened a qualified institutions placement on 11 Nov with a floor price of ₹992.25 a share and the option of a discount of up to 5%. Reuters, via BusinessLine, says the board approved up to ₹11,700 crore through the QIP in October; it comes a day after the ₹5,799 crore warrant allotment to Bajaj Finserv.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: two large NBFCs are raising fresh equity in the same week. Muthoot Fincorp's proposed ₹3,000 crore IPO is likely to be compared against freshly capitalised lenders.
Sources: [NSE filing](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/BAJFINANCE_11112026171309_SEintimationopeningofissue.pdf) · [BusinessLine (Reuters)](https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/markets/bajaj-finance-launches-11700-crore-share-sale-sets-floor-price-at-99225/article71690436.ece) · [CNBC-TV18](https://www.cnbctv18.com/market/bajaj-finance-launches-qip-with-floor-price-at-rs992-25-per-share-20031765.htm)

### IIFL Finance calls an EGM for 4 Dec; Nirmal Jain puts gold-loan share at about 3%
<!-- story: id=iifl-finance-blackstone-raise-2026 -->
<!-- tags: companies=iifl-finance; topics=funding,gold-loan,m-and-a; category=deals -->
IIFL Finance has called an extra-ordinary general meeting for 4 December 2026 by video conference, with 27 November as the e-voting cut-off date, to approve the preferential issue. Founder and MD Nirmal Jain told CNBC-TV18 that about 70% of the ₹6,414 crore will go to fresh disbursements and 30% to repaying loans, that its cost of funds is around 9.4%, and that he expects a 25–50 bp cut in borrowing costs in FY28.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: Jain put IIFL's gold-loan book at about ₹65,000 crore, around 3% of a ₹22 lakh crore market, and said leading AA+ gold-loan companies borrow 120–150 bp cheaper. A rating upgrade for IIFL after the deal would mean a better-funded competitor in the gold-loan markets where Muthoot Fincorp operates.
Sources: [NSE: IIFL EGM notice](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/IIFL_12112026004505_SE_Intimation.pdf) · [CNBC-TV18 interview](https://www.cnbctv18.com/business/companies/exclusive-iifl-finance-nirmal-jain-%e2%82%b95000-crore-profit-target-by-fy29-loan-growth-balance-sheet-fundraise-20031218.htm) · [NSE bhavcopy](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/products/content/sec_bhavdata_full_11112026.csv)

## 🔄 Deals & M&A

### Foreign capital keeps flowing into NBFCs, Mint reports
<!-- story: id=nbfc-foreign-investment-2026; continues=iifl-finance-blackstone-raise-2026 -->
<!-- tags: companies=iifl-finance,shriram-finance,manappuram; topics=funding,m-and-a; category=deals -->
Mint lists the recent foreign stakes in Indian lenders: Blackstone's ₹5,000 crore in IIFL, TPG's Rise Funds' ₹525 crore (up to ₹800 crore) in Arka Fincap, MUFG's ₹39,618 crore for 20% of Shriram Finance and Bain's 18% of Manappuram. Equirus Capital's Nirav Shah told Mint that even a 50 bp cut in borrowing costs makes a significant difference to NBFC profitability.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: two listed gold lenders, Manappuram (Bain) and IIFL (Blackstone), now have a large foreign financial investor, and Mint notes that rating upgrades have followed such deals. That is the backdrop for Muthoot Fincorp's proposed IPO.

## 🏘️ Sector Deep Dive — Thursday: MSME & commercial lending

### ICRA upgrades micro-enterprise lender Aye Finance to A+
<!-- story: id=aye-finance-icra-upgrade-2026; single_source=official -->
<!-- tags: companies=aye-finance; topics=rating,asset-quality; category=ratings -->
ICRA upgraded Aye Finance to [ICRA]A+ (Stable) from [ICRA]A, its second upgrade this financial year after India Ratings in June, the company said in an 11 Nov exchange filing. Aye reports AUM of ₹7,597 crore at 30 September, up 26% year on year, gross Stage 3 down to 4.2% from 4.8% in March, and capital adequacy of 41%.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: Aye lends to micro enterprises, a segment Muthoot Fincorp also serves with business loans; a higher rating and 41% capital adequacy give Aye more room to grow in it.
Sources: [NSE: Aye Finance press release](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/AYEFINANCE_11112026141327_AYE_PressRelease.pdf)
