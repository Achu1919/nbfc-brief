## 🔔 New Regulatory Updates Today

Two RBI circulars dated **27 Oct** are addressed to NBFCs. Both are routine sanctions-list updates that require regulated entities to update their sanctions screening. SEBI issued one debt-market circular, and it is aimed at debenture trustees, not issuers. Nothing new from RBI drafts or IBBI.

### 1. [CIRCULAR] UAPA s51A: UNSC 1267/1989 ISIL & Al-Qaida list, **removal of 3 entries**
- **Reference:** `RBI/2026-27/301` · `DoR.AML.REC.253/14.06.001/2026-27` · **27 Oct 2026**
- **Addressed to:** banks of all types, **Non-Banking Financial Companies**, ARCs and AIFIs.
- **What it says:** MEA has relayed UNSC press release **SC/16488 (26 Oct 2026)**. Three entries have been removed from the ISIL (Da'esh) & Al-Qaida Sanctions List, so the assets freeze, travel ban and arms embargo no longer apply to them. The circular refers back to **Chapter IX of the KYC Directions, 2025**.
- [RBI](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=13747&Mode=0)

### 2. [CIRCULAR] UAPA s51A: same list, **amendment of 3 entries**
- **Reference:** `RBI/2026-27/302` · `DoR.AML.REC.254/14.06.001/2026-27` · **27 Oct 2026**
- **What it says:** UNSC press release **SC/16490 (26 Oct 2026)** changes identifiers on 3 listed entries, such as aliases and passport details. **These entries are still sanctioned.**
- [RBI](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=13748&Mode=0)

### 3. [CONTEXT] SEBI: debenture trustees get until **27 Apr 2027** to ring-fence non-SEBI business
- **Reference:** SEBI circular `HO/(201)2026-DDHS-POD1/I/24467/2026` · **27 Oct 2026**
- **What it says:**
  - Regulation 9C of the DT Regulations (notified 27 Oct 2025) lets DTs move non-SEBI activities into separate business units (SBUs).
  - The deadline had already been extended once, to 27 Oct 2026. It now moves out by six months because SEBI's Expert Working Group (set up in June 2026) is reviewing the whole DT framework, SBUs included.
  - All other provisions of the 25 Nov 2025 circular stay unchanged.
- **Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp:** none directly. The circular applies to debenture trustees, not to issuers such as Muthoot Fincorp.
- [SEBI](https://www.sebi.gov.in/legal/circulars/oct-2026/extension-of-timeline-for-compliance-with-terms-and-conditions-by-debenture-trustees-dts-for-carrying-out-activities-outside-the-purview-of-sebi_105454.html)
