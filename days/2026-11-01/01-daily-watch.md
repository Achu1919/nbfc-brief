## 🔴 URGENT / High Impact

**1. Muthoot Finance grew fast in Q2 and raised its gold-loan guidance, but its bad loans jumped.**
- Standalone Q2 FY27 PAT **₹2,977 cr (+27%)**; consolidated PAT **₹3,278 cr (+36%)**. Standalone loan AUM **₹1,87,810 cr**, up 42% year on year and 9% quarter on quarter; standalone gold-loan AUM ₹1,78,956 cr (+43%). Results were declared on Saturday, 31 Oct. ([NDTV Profit](https://www.ndtvprofit.com/markets/muthoot-finance-q2-results-profit-rises-27-to-rs-2-977-crore-revises-gold-loan-growth-guidance-for-fy27-12263278), [CNBC-TV18](https://www.cnbctv18.com/market/earnings/muthoot-finance-q2-results-net-profit-rises-27-to-rs2978-crore-nii-up-34-beats-estimates-20024053.htm), [ET](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/markets/stocks/earnings/muthoot-finance-q2-cons-profit-jumps-36-yoy-to-rs-3278-crore/articleshow/135401237.cms))
- MD Alexander George Muthoot said the company is raising FY27 gold-loan growth guidance "by an additional 10%", and that the RBI's gold-lending rules effective 1 April 2026 involve a learning curve for the company as well as for customers, which he expects to stabilise over a few quarters. ([NDTV Profit](https://www.ndtvprofit.com/markets/muthoot-finance-q2-results-profit-rises-27-to-rs-2-977-crore-revises-gold-loan-growth-guidance-for-fy27-12263278))
- Standalone gross NPA (Stage 3) rose to **3.87% from 2.28%** three months earlier. ([ET](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/markets/stocks/earnings/muthoot-finance-q2-cons-profit-jumps-36-yoy-to-rs-3278-crore/articleshow/135401237.cms)) Cofacto's read of the filing puts the quarter's impairment charge at ₹514.92 cr and provision coverage at 11.80%. ([Cofacto](https://cofacto.ai/market-news/muthootfin-q2-fy27-results-analysis))
> **Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp:** This is the closest public read on what the new RBI gold-loan rules did to a large gold-loan book in Q2. A Stage 3 rise of about 160 bp in one quarter at the largest player is likely to prompt similar questions for other gold lenders, including Muthoot Fincorp, when their Q2 numbers come out.

**2. Gold-loan stress is now showing up across the sector, not at one lender.**
- Shriram Finance said on 23 Oct that gross Stage 3 in its gold book rose to **3.12% from 2.57%** a quarter earlier. Bajaj Finance flagged a rise in 30+ day overdues partly driven by short-term gold loans it has stopped offering, and Piramal Finance called fast gold-loan growth an emerging risk. ([VCCircle/Reuters](https://www.vccircle.com/shriramfinance-joins-peers-in-flagging-stress-in-gold-loans))
> **Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp:** Three diversified lenders plus Muthoot Finance now point the same way. The common thread in the coverage is the April 2026 rules (renewal only after interest is cleared, bullet tenor caps), which push some loans overdue that used to be rolled, so the split between technical overdue and real collection problems is likely to matter in comparisons across gold lenders, including Muthoot Fincorp.


**3. Gold steadied this morning after Friday's 3% fall; oil is still near $118.**
- Spot gold was **$3,875.40 (+0.06%)** at 07:10 IST today, after falling 3.04% to $3,899.33 on Friday. ([exa.ai XAUUSD](https://exa.ai/library/markets/commodity/gold?currency=USD), [CNBC-TV18](https://www.cnbctv18.com/market/commodities/gold-silver-prices-crash-mcx-gold-plunges-to-rs-1-lakh-silver-falls-to-rs-1-lakh-per-kg-20023794.htm))

> **Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp:** Collateral value is roughly where it was on Friday night. Oil keeps a December Fed hike priced in, which feeds NBFC borrowing costs, including Muthoot Fincorp's.

## 🏭 Sector Deep Dive — Sunday: Weekly preview + sector health check

**1. The week ahead.**
- **Mon 2 Nov:** first trading day after Muthoot Finance's results; US PMI. **Tue 3 Nov:** US midterm elections; SBI, Bajaj Finserv and MCX results. **Wed 4 Nov:** BSE results. **Thu 5 Nov:** LIC and Titan results; Muthoot Fincorp Tranche I NCD issue closes; Muthoot Microfin board. **Fri 6 Nov:** US jobs data. - Titan's numbers are the best read on retail gold-jewellery demand at these prices, which is the other side of the gold-loan collateral story.

**2. Health check: growth is as strong as expected.**
- Before the season, JM Financial expected aggregate NBFC AUM growth of about 20% and 27% for gold, MSME and LAP lenders. ([ETBFSI](https://bfsi.economictimes.indiatimes.com/articles/nbfc-aum-growth-seen-at-20-in-q2fy27-funding-costs-emerge-as-key-risk/134720832)) Muthoot Finance's 42% standalone AUM growth ran well ahead of that.

**3. Health check: asset quality is where the surprise came.**
- The same preview expected only a "marginal increase in credit costs". ([ETBFSI](https://bfsi.economictimes.indiatimes.com/articles/nbfc-aum-growth-seen-at-20-in-q2fy27-funding-costs-emerge-as-key-risk/134720832)) Instead gold-loan Stage 3 rose at Muthoot Finance and Shriram, and Bajaj and Piramal flagged gold-loan risk. ([ET](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/markets/stocks/earnings/muthoot-finance-q2-cons-profit-jumps-36-yoy-to-rs-3278-crore/articleshow/135401237.cms), [VCCircle/Reuters](https://www.vccircle.com/shriramfinance-joins-peers-in-flagging-stress-in-gold-loans))

**4. Health check: funding is the slow squeeze.**
- JM Financial noted three- and five-year AAA NBFC bond yields up 50–60 bp since June. ([ETBFSI](https://bfsi.economictimes.indiatimes.com/articles/nbfc-aum-growth-seen-at-20-in-q2fy27-funding-costs-emerge-as-key-risk/134720832)) The benchmark 10-year G-sec closed at 7.3902% on Thursday, though it eased about 8.5 bp over the week, and markets put the odds of a December Fed hike at about 83%. ([BusinessLine live, 30 Oct](https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/markets/sensex-nifty50-today-stock-market-highlights-30th-october-2026/article71640574.ece))

> **Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp:** The market is rewarding gold-loan growth, but this week the questions for gold lenders, including Muthoot Fincorp, are about Stage 3 and how the April rules flow through it. Gold is about 3% below Thursday's level and steady overnight.
