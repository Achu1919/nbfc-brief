### SEBI Credit Risk-o-Meter comes into force today
<!-- reg: ref=HO/17/11/22(1)2026-DDHS-POD1/I/23092/2026; regulator=SEBI; issued=2026-10-07; effective=2026-11-21; status=in-force; lifecycle=effective; applies=nbfcs,intermediaries -->
<!-- story: id=reg-sebi-ho-17-11-22-1-2026-ddhs-pod1; single_source=official; milestone=effective -->
SEBI circular HO/17/11/22(1)2026-DDHS-POD1/I/23092/2026, dated 7 October 2026, introduced the Credit Risk-o-Meter as a mandatory colour-coded disclosure for debt securities. Its paragraph 5 says the provisions "shall come into force after 45 days from the date of issuance" — that is today, 21 November 2026. From today the meter is a mandatory disclosure in offer documents, abridged prospectuses, private placement memoranda, issuer and online bond platform provider (OBPP) advertisements, and OBPP web and mobile platforms. The circular inserts a new Chapter II-C in the NCS Master Circular dated 15 October 2025 and a new clause 14 in Annexure-XXIB, the OBPP minimum disclosures. Its scope covers all listed and proposed-to-be-listed non-convertible securities, commercial papers, securitised debt instruments, security receipts and structured debt/MLDs, whether issued by public issue or private placement. The meter carries six colour-coded risk levels mapped from AAA to D (A1+ to D for short-term ratings), and where several credit rating agencies rate an issue, the lowest rating applies. OBPPs must update the meter within 24 hours of a rating-change intimation from NSDL or CDSL, with automated systems, no manual overrides and audit trails.

Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: Muthoot Fincorp's listed NCDs (Tranche I is listed on BSE) fall inside the circular's scope for any future offer document, private placement memorandum and advertisement from today. The circular is a rule now in force, not a representation, and it changes no term of an already-listed issue.
