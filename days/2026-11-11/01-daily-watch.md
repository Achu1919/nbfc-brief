# 📊 NBFC Daily Watch — Wednesday, November 11, 2026

Morning edition for Wednesday, 11 November 2026 (IST). Covers Tuesday 10 Nov, a trading holiday on NSE and BSE, and overnight. Stock and index closes below are still Monday's, the latest official files.

## 🔴 URGENT / High Impact

### Muthoot Fincorp's Tranche I NCDs start trading on BSE today
<!-- tags: companies=muthoot-fincorp; topics=ncd,funding; category=muthoot-fincorp -->
BSE notice 20261109-30, dated 9 Nov, says Muthoot Fincorp's secured redeemable NCDs are admitted to dealings from Wednesday 11 November 2026 in the "F" group, in demat form with a market lot of one bond. The notice names Integrated Registry Management Services as registrar.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: the listing date for the issue is now set; the deemed date of allotment and the final allotted amount had not been seen in a published source as of this edition.
Source: [BSE notice 20261109-30](https://www.bseindia.com/downloads/UploadDocs/Notices/20261109-30/20261109-30.pdf)

### Update: IIFL Finance board approves a ₹6,414 crore raise led by Blackstone
<!-- tags: companies=iifl-finance; topics=funding,m-and-a,gold-loan; category=deals -->
IIFL's board approved on 10 Nov a preferential issue of CCPS and warrants at ₹602 each: Blackstone funds put in ₹5,000 crore for about 16% fully diluted, promoters ₹1,264 crore through warrants and Anchorage Capital ₹150 crore. ET says IIFL expects its capital adequacy to rise from about 17% to about 30% pro forma for September 2026, and that it plans to use the money to grow secured lending, particularly gold finance.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: IIFL Finance is a listed gold-loan competitor that has now stated a gold-loan growth plan backed by fresh equity, subject to shareholder and regulatory approvals; the ₹602 issue price is also a recent valuation reference for a gold-focused NBFC ahead of Muthoot Fincorp's proposed IPO.
Source: [Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/markets/stocks/news/iifl-finance-to-raise-rs-6414-crore-through-preferential-issue-blackstone-to-invest-rs-5000-crore/articleshow/135711113.cms)

### Gold falls about 2.7% after a hot US inflation print
<!-- tags: topics=gold-price,macro,markets; category=markets -->
Reuters reported spot gold down 2.7% at $4,028.39 an ounce at 1:53 pm ET on Tuesday, after US CPI rose 0.4% in October against a 0.3% forecast. Bets on a 50 bp Fed hike in December rose to about 10% from zero, according to CME FedWatch as cited by Reuters.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: IBJA's 10 Nov AM rate was published before the fall, and AGLOC's 11 Nov per-gram ceilings are unchanged; a lower rupee gold price would reduce collateral values across gold-loan books, including Muthoot Fincorp's.
Source: [Reuters via Mint](https://www.livemint.com/market/gold-falls-nearly-3-as-cpi-tops-forecasts-lifting-dollar-and-yields-11794337899940.html)

## 🔄 Deals & M&A

### Bajaj Finance allots ₹5,799 crore of warrants to Bajaj Finserv
<!-- tags: companies=bajaj-finance; topics=funding; category=deals -->
A board committee of Bajaj Finance allotted 5,74,25,740 warrants to promoter Bajaj Finserv on 10 Nov at ₹1,010 each, about ₹5,799.9 crore in all, PTI reported. A quarter of the money is payable at allotment and the rest when the warrants are converted into shares.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: two large listed NBFCs added fresh equity on the same day, which forms part of the peer backdrop for Muthoot Fincorp's proposed ₹3,000 crore IPO.

## 💻 Digital & Fintech

### Piramal Finance CEO says the new co-lending rules may end upfront-monetisation models
<!-- tags: companies=piramal-finance; topics=digital-lending,funding; category=digital -->
In an ETBFSI interview published on 10 Nov, Piramal Finance MD and CEO Jairam Sridharan said originators that book the present value of future co-lending cash flows as upfront profit may not survive the new framework. He said Piramal's co-lending book is about ₹5,000 crore, growing 20–25% a year, and that it participates mainly as the investor.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: Muthoot Microfin has reported gold loans sourced through referrals and co-lending with Muthoot Fincorp, so the treatment of co-lent loans is relevant to both companies.
Source: [ETBFSI](https://bfsi.economictimes.indiatimes.com/articles/piramal-finance-ceo-says-new-co-lending-norms-may-impact-upfront-loan-monetisation-models/135697169)

## 🏘️ Sector Deep Dive — Wednesday: Housing finance

### HFCs turn to construction finance for yield
<!-- tags: companies=lic-housing-finance,pnb-housing-finance; topics=housing-finance; category=sector -->
Financial Express reports LIC Housing Finance expects its project-loan book, ₹9,740 crore at 30 September, to reach ₹15,000 crore by March, and PNB Housing has restarted construction finance with a ₹10,000–11,000 crore target over three years. FE cites RBI data showing NBFC and HFC credit to commercial real estate up 23.4% year on year to ₹1,05,845 crore in September 2026, with construction-finance yields of 13–15%.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: its subsidiary Muthoot Housing Finance operates in retail housing finance, the segment FE describes as thin-margin.
Source: [Financial Express](https://www.financialexpress.com/business/banking-finance-hfcs-lean-towards-realty-construction-finance-4380301/)

### Godrej Housing Finance targets a ₹10,000 crore affordable book by 2031
<!-- tags: companies=godrej-housing-finance; topics=housing-finance; category=sector -->
Godrej Housing Finance told Mint on 9 Nov that its affordable-housing AUM is ₹600 crore across 52 branches, with an average ticket under ₹20 lakh, and that it wants ₹10,000 crore by 2031. Its total AUM has crossed ₹10,000 crore, of which prime home loans are ₹8,500 crore.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: a group-backed lender expanding affordable housing in tier 2 and 3 towns adds competition in the segment served by its subsidiary Muthoot Housing Finance.
Source: [Mint](https://www.livemint.com/companies/news/godrej-housing-finance-targets-10-000-crore-affordable-loan-book-by-2031-11794200509961.html)
