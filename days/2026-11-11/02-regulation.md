## 🔔 New Regulatory Updates Today

✅ No new RBI, SEBI, IBBI, NHB or FIU-IND instrument for NBFCs on 10 Nov, a market holiday. One exchange notice concerns Muthoot Fincorp: its NCDs list today.

### BSE admits Muthoot Fincorp's Tranche I NCDs to dealings from 11 Nov
<!-- tags: companies=muthoot-fincorp; topics=ncd,funding; category=muthoot-fincorp -->
BSE notice 20261109-30 (DCS-Listing, 9 Nov) admits Muthoot Fincorp's secured redeemable NCDs to dealings from 11 Nov 2026 in the "F" group, demat only, market lot of one bond. It's an exchange notice, not a circular, so it gets no reg block.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: the issue's listing step is complete; the deemed date of allotment and final allotted amount had not been seen in a published source as of this edition.
Source: [BSE notice 20261109-30](https://www.bseindia.com/downloads/UploadDocs/Notices/20261109-30/20261109-30.pdf)

Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: no new regulatory item. Dates already on the calendar include the 21 Nov Credit Risk-o-Meter start and the 26 Nov comment deadline on RBI's draft PSL Directions.
