### RBI: two UAPA sanctions-list amendments under Section 51A — a screening obligation, not a lending rule
<!-- reg: regulator=RBI; issued=2026-11-26; status=new; lifecycle=final; applies=nbfcs,banks -->
<!-- story: id=reg-rbi-uapa-51a-2026-11 -->
The RBI's notifications listing, read 07:02 IST, carries a newest block dated **26 November 2026** with two items on the same subject: **"Implementation of Section 51A of UAPA, 1967: Updates to UNSC's 1267/1989 ISIL (Da'esh) & Al-Qaida Sanctions List: Amendment of 01 Entry"**, and the same heading with **"Amendment of 81 entries"**. Both are sanctions-list maintenance under the Unlawful Activities (Prevention) Act: the amendments add to and amend entries on the UNSC's consolidated sanctions list, and regulated entities are bound to screen customers and transactions against the updated list. **That is the whole of their reach — a KYC/screening obligation update. They do not change any gold-loan rule, any LTV band, any auction practice, any pricing norm or any other lending obligation, and this report does not inflate them into one.** No reference number for either notification is visible in the listing read this morning, so none is stated here.

Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: the company screens customers like any RBI-regulated entity, and an updated sanctions list is an input to that screening; no lending rule applying to the company changed today.

## 📖 The standing rulebook: the LTV bands, unchanged; AGLOC's 27 November block rises
<!-- story: id=reg-rbi-rbi-2025-26-47 -->
The gold-loan LTV bands that Thursday's auction-notice reporting rests on are the standing ones: **85% for loans up to ₹2.5 lakh, 80% for loans between ₹2.5 and 5 lakh, 75% above ₹5 lakh.** **No RBI instrument dated 26 or 27 November changes them, and no instrument of any regulator dated those days changes any other rule applying to Muthoot Fincorp's lending business.** What did move is the published ceiling inside the bands: **AGLOC's block dated 27 November 2026 puts the bands at ₹9,548 a gram at 85%, ₹8,987 at 80% and ₹8,425 at 75%, on a jewellery reference of ₹11,233** — against the 26 November block of ₹9,424 / ₹8,869 / ₹8,315 on a reference of ₹11,087, that is **+₹124, +₹118 and +₹110, with the reference up ₹146** — the second rise in the series since 17 November, after the one on 24 November, against the falls recorded on 19, 20, 21, 23 and 26 November. The reference is fresh, not carried forward: **₹11,233 is IBJA's 26 November PM 916 rate ÷ 10 (11,233.9)**, so today's block rests on Thursday afternoon's bullion print exactly as the 26 November block rested on the 25 November one. **All three bands move together because they are fixed percentages of the one reference — one observation, not three** — and the percentages on those changes are arithmetic on AGLOC's own two blocks, because the page states rupee figures only.

One representation sits next to the bands today and is not a rule: the Economic Times reported on 26 November that **banks and gold-loan companies such as Muthoot and Manappuram have informed the RBI that, as a prudential measure, they have lowered the LTV on fresh loans to an average of 60–65% from about 75%.** ET names "Muthoot" without saying which entity — Muthoot Finance is the listed company, and Muthoot Fincorp is not named — and a representation to the RBI is not a rule change: **the bands stand, and lenders writing below them is prudential discretion, not a regulatory requirement on any named company.** It is written here as reported, not as a rule.

Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: these bands and this reference govern the ceiling the company lends under — ₹9,548 at the 85% band today, ₹124 higher than yesterday's. The fresh reference is a market print, not a rule move; the 60–65% figure is an industry representation reported by ET, not an obligation; and nothing in either changes the company's rulebook today.
