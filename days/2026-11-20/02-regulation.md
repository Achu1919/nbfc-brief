## ⚖️ Enforcement action (not a rule change)

RBI's 20 Nov releases carried one enforcement item. By an order dated 17 Nov 2026, RBI imposed a monetary penalty of ₹7.50 lakh on Fusion Finance Limited for non-compliance with its KYC directions and its Internal Ombudsman directions. The order says the company failed to obtain PAN, an equivalent e-document or Form 60 from customers when establishing an account-based relationship, and failed to escalate partly or wholly rejected complaints to the Internal Ombudsman for a final decision within the prescribed time. It follows RBI's statutory inspection of the company as on 31 March 2026 and is stated to rest on deficiencies in regulatory compliance. The rest of the 20 Nov releases were operational: a 4-day VRR notified for 23 Nov, the result of a second 3-day VRR, conversion of state government securities, Treasury Bill auctions, the weekly statistical supplement and FAQs on the FEMA export and import regulations.

Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: none directly, since the order is against another lender and changes no rule. The Internal Ombudsman obligation it cites is the one behind Muthoot Fincorp's own Oct–Dec quarterly return, due 15 Jan 2027.
Sources: [RBI press release 2026-2027/1537, penalty on Fusion Finance, 20 Nov](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx?prid=64015) · [RBI press release 2026-2027/1531, FEMA FAQs, 20 Nov](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx?prid=64009) · [RBI press releases, 20 Nov](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx)
