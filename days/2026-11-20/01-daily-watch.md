## 🔴 URGENT / High Impact

### Gold-loan lenders lag a record market day as gold resumes its fall
<!-- story: id=nbfc-gold-loan-divergence-2026-11-19 -->
<!-- tags: companies=muthoot-finance,manappuram,iifl-finance; topics=gold-loan,gold-price,markets; category=gold -->
On Thursday 19 Nov the Sensex rose 989.94 points (+1.21%) to 83,021.75, and Nifty Bank closed at a record 63,063.00 (+1.65%). The gold-loan lenders did not take part. Muthoot Finance (a listed peer, and not Muthoot Fincorp) fell 0.83% to ₹2,618.00, its second straight decline. Manappuram Finance slipped 0.05% to ₹282.85, and IIFL Finance fell 2.64% to ₹644.45. The Nifty NBFC index rose 0.04%, against 1.10% for Nifty Financial Services.

The gold price was the backdrop. MCX December gold futures settled at ₹1,29,770 per 10 g, down ₹44 (−0.03%). Comex December gold settled at $3,685.50 an ounce, down 1.51%, after a 3.16% fall the previous session. AGLOC's 85% LTV ceiling for 20 Nov fell ₹127 a gram to ₹10,085, and its jewellery reference fell ₹149 to ₹11,865. IBJA's 999 rate for the 19 Nov PM session was ₹12,953.1 a gram, down ₹163.3.

This is how the collateral mechanism works. A gold loan is capped at a share of the value of the gold pledged, the loan-to-value (LTV) ratio. AGLOC publishes that cap as a rupee figure per gram for each band: ₹10,085 at 85%, ₹9,492 at 80% and ₹8,898 at 75%, all set against a jewellery reference of ₹11,865. That reference equals IBJA's 916 (22-carat) rate for the 19 Nov PM session. When gold falls, the per-gram figures fall with it. For a loan already written close to its ceiling, the cushion becomes thinner. For fresh lending against the same grams, the maximum loan is smaller. The same pledged gold supports less debt than it did a day earlier.

Moneycontrol's 19 Nov report places the lenders' fall alongside the gold price. Its text says gold loan shares fell up to 2.6% on Thursday, and that gold prices remained near a 14-month low. The same report says Muthoot Finance has lost 31.8% year to date and Manappuram 10%, while IIFL Finance is up 4.1% for the year.

Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: a lower gold price lowers the per-gram value of the collateral behind every gold loan, Muthoot Fincorp's included. Loans written close to an LTV ceiling have a thinner cushion, and the same grams support less new lending. The sources checked give no Muthoot Fincorp loan-book or LTV figure for 19 Nov.
