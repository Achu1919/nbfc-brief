# 📊 NBFC Daily Watch — Tuesday, November 10, 2026

Morning edition for Tuesday, 10 November 2026 (IST). Covers Monday 9 Nov and overnight. Today is a trading holiday on NSE and BSE (Diwali-Balipratipada), so the closes below are Monday's.

## 🔴 URGENT / High Impact

### Blackstone set to take 15% of IIFL Finance for about ₹5,000 crore
<!-- tags: companies=iifl-finance; topics=funding,m-and-a,gold-loan; category=deals -->
ET reports that Blackstone will invest around ₹5,000 crore for 15% of IIFL Finance, with promoters Nirmal Jain and R Venkataraman adding ₹1,200 crore, taking the total infusion to a little over ₹6,200 crore. ET says the money would lift IIFL's capital adequacy by about 5 percentage points to 28% and could cut its borrowing costs by 100–150 bps through a better rating; Moneycontrol reported the board meets on 10 Nov to weigh the proposals.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: IIFL Finance is a listed gold-loan competitor, and a better-capitalised IIFL with lower funding costs could compete more strongly on gold-loan pricing; the deal is also a fresh private-equity valuation reference for gold-focused NBFCs ahead of Muthoot Fincorp's proposed IPO.
Source: [Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/industry/banking/finance/blackstone-set-to-acquire-15-of-iifl-for-rs-5000-crore/articleshow/135691311.cms)

### CII asks the Finance Ministry to review the 100% risk weight on secured gold loans
<!-- tags: topics=gold-loan,policy,funding; category=regulation -->
CNBC-TV18 reports that CII's reform agenda asks for a review of the 100% risk weight on secured gold loans, easier ECB conditions for NBFCs, and more access to long-term money from HNIs, pension funds and insurers. It also asks the government to look at the tax gap between banks and NBFCs.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: this is an industry proposal, not a rule change; a lower risk weight on gold loans, if adopted, would reduce the capital held against gold-loan books such as Muthoot Fincorp's.
Source: [CNBC-TV18](https://www.cnbctv18.com/economy/cii-recommends-regulatory-relaxations-nbfcs-reform-agenda-to-finance-ministry20030393-20030393.htm)

## 🔄 Deals & M&A

### Satya Micro Capital plans to sell its housing finance arm for ₹150–200 crore
<!-- tags: companies=satya-micro-capital; topics=microfinance,housing-finance,m-and-a,funding; category=deals -->
Satya Micro Capital's MD told ET the NBFC-MFI has shortlisted three of seven or eight suitors for Satya Micro Housing Finance, which has a ₹260 crore loan book, and wants to close within a month. ET says the lender must repay about ₹175 crore by end-December, and its AUM fell below ₹2,000 crore by June from ₹6,000 crore in April 2024.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: an asset sale to meet repayments points to continued funding strain at weaker microfinance lenders, the segment where Muthoot Fincorp's subsidiary Muthoot Microfin operates.
Source: [Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/industry/banking/finance/satya-micro-capital-to-sell-its-housing-finance-arm-to-address-liquidity-crunch/articleshow/135684200.cms)

## 🏭 Sector Deep Dive — Tuesday: Gold loan sector

### Gold lenders rally with gold on Monday; MCX December gold up 1.26% by late morning
<!-- tags: companies=muthoot-finance,manappuram,iifl-finance; topics=gold-loan,gold-price,markets; category=sector -->
Moneycontrol reported Muthoot Finance up 3% at ₹2,938.50 at 11:45 am on 9 Nov, with MCX December gold up ₹1,801 (1.26%) to ₹1,44,353 per 10g. By the close the gains had narrowed, with Muthoot Finance up 2.19% and Manappuram up 0.23% on NSE's bhavcopy.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: gold-lender shares continue to track gold prices day to day; AGLOC's per-gram ceiling is unchanged for 10 Nov.
Source: [Moneycontrol](https://www.moneycontrol.com/news/business/markets/muthoot-finance-manappuram-shares-gain-up-to-3-as-gold-prices-rebound-14075180.html)

### Three things now shaping gold-lender capital: IIFL's raise, CII's risk-weight ask, and AGLOC's flat ceiling
<!-- tags: companies=iifl-finance; topics=gold-loan,funding,policy; category=sector -->
IIFL is set to raise over ₹6,200 crore in fresh equity, CII is asking the government to review the 100% risk weight on gold loans, and AGLOC's 85% ceiling is unchanged at ₹11,013/g for 10 Nov. The first two are about how much capital each rupee of gold loan uses; the third is about how much each gram can carry.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: competition is most direct in loans below ₹2.5 lakh, where the 85% LTV tier applies.
Source: [Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/industry/banking/finance/blackstone-set-to-acquire-15-of-iifl-for-rs-5000-crore/articleshow/135691311.cms)
