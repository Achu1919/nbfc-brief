## 🔔 New Regulatory Updates Today

One RBI enforcement item (a registration cancellation) and no new circular for NBFCs. Today is an exchange holiday.

### RBI cancels the Certificate of Registration of Gandhinagar Leasing and Finance Ltd
<!-- tags: companies=gandhinagar-leasing-and-finance; regulators=rbi; topics=enforcement; category=regulation -->
<!-- reg: ref=Press Release 2026-2027/1471; regulator=RBI; applies=nbfcs; issued=2026-11-09; effective=2026-10-29; status=in-force; title=CoR of Gandhinagar Leasing and Finance Ltd cancelled -->
RBI said on 9 Nov that it cancelled the Certificate of Registration (CoR No. 01.00279, dated 8 Dec 1998) of the Ahmedabad-based NBFC on 29 Oct 2026, under Section 45-IA(6) of the RBI Act. The company can no longer carry on the business of a non-banking financial institution.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: none directly; the release covers a small Gujarat NBFC and gives no reason for the cancellation.
Reference: Press Release 2026-2027/1471 · Addressed to: Gandhinagar Leasing and Finance Ltd (public notice) · Issued: 9 Nov 2026 · Effective: 29 Oct 2026
Source: [RBI](https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx?prid=63949)

### CII asks for a review of the 100% risk weight on gold loans (industry proposal, not a rule)
<!-- tags: topics=gold-loan,policy,funding; category=regulation -->
CNBC-TV18 reports that CII's reform agenda to the Finance Ministry asks for a review of the 100% risk weight on secured gold loans, easier ECB conditions for NBFCs, and lighter governance and dividend rules for Upper Layer NBFCs. Nothing has been issued by RBI or the government.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: no change today; a lower risk weight on gold loans, if adopted, would raise the capital ratio of gold lenders such as Muthoot Fincorp for the same loan book.
Reference: industry proposal (no circular) · Addressed to: Ministry of Finance · Issued: 9 Nov 2026 (press report)
Source: [CNBC-TV18](https://www.cnbctv18.com/economy/cii-recommends-regulatory-relaxations-nbfcs-reform-agenda-to-finance-ministry20030393-20030393.htm)

### Watchlist sweep: nothing new for NBFCs on 9–10 Nov
RBI's notifications feed still ends with the 5 Nov Master Directions on cyber resilience for non-bank payment system operators. Its 9 Nov press releases are the CoR cancellation above plus routine items (G-sec and SDL auctions, a VRR result, money-market operations and an Abhyudaya Co-operative Bank board supersession extension). SEBI's feed has nothing after 6 Nov, IBBI's site still shows a last update of 31 Oct, and NHB's notifications page lists no notice dated after 4 Nov.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: none from the regulators today. The live items are still the 14 Nov Reg 52 results, the 21 Nov Credit Risk-o-Meter start and the 26 Nov PSL comment window.
Source: [RBI press releases](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx)
