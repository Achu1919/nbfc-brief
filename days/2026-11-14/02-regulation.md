### RBI fines One Mobikwik Systems ₹9 lakh over PPI transfers and fraud reporting
<!-- story: id=rbi-mobikwik-penalty-2026-11; dup; single_source=official -->
<!-- tags: companies=mobikwik; regulators=rbi; topics=enforcement; category=regulation -->
By an order dated 6 November 2026, RBI imposed a ₹9 lakh penalty on One Mobikwik Systems under the Payment and Settlement Systems Act for letting certain PPI holders transfer funds to non-pre-registered beneficiaries beyond the limit and for failing to report certain frauds on time. The inspection covered June 2025 to March 2026.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: not an NBFC rule. The fraud-reporting charge is a reminder that RBI checks reporting timelines line by line in inspections, a requirement that also applies to NBFCs' own fraud returns.
Sources: [RBI press release](https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx?prid=63973)
