Morning edition for Saturday, 14 November 2026 (IST). Covers Friday 13 Nov and overnight. Stock and index closes are Friday's NSE files; markets are shut today.

## 🔴 URGENT / High Impact

### Gold lenders pull back from short-tenure and EMI gold loans after Q2 slippage
<!-- story: id=gold-loan-asset-quality-2026 -->
<!-- tags: companies=bajaj-finance,manappuram,muthoot-finance,shriram-finance; topics=gold-loan,asset-quality; category=asset-quality -->
The Economic Times reports that Bajaj Finance stopped short-tenure gold schemes in August and Manappuram is moving customers back to one-year loans, after borrowers used to bullet repayment fell behind on monthly-interest and EMI products launched under RBI's April 2026 gold-lending rules. Muthoot Finance's standalone Stage 2 rose to ₹23,467 crore at September-end from ₹1,876 crore in June, and Manappuram's gold Stage 2 went to about 5% from 0.95% in the quarter, per CNBC-TV18.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: the whole listed peer set now says the same thing on the call: periodic-payment gold products slip into Stage 2 fast even when the gold cover is fine. Anyone reading Muthoot Fincorp's Q2 numbers will look first at how much of its gold book sits in monthly-interest or EMI products and what its Stage 2 did in the quarter.
Sources: [Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/satet-front/dodging-bullet-loans-hurt-gold-nbfcs/articleshow/135817183.cms) · [CNBC-TV18](https://www.cnbctv18.com/market/manappuram-finance-share-price-q2-result-reaction-asset-quality-gold-stage-2-loans-clsa-upgrade-buy-target-risks-20033253.htm)

### AGLOC cuts the 85% ceiling again, to ₹10,574 a gram for 14–16 Nov
<!-- story: id=gold-price-weekly -->
<!-- tags: topics=gold-price,gold-loan,macro; category=markets -->
AGLOC's 85% LTV ceiling for 14–16 Nov is ₹10,574 a gram, down ₹146 from 13 Nov, on a jewellery reference price of ₹12,440 (was ₹12,612). IBJA's 13 Nov PM rate for 999 gold was ₹1,35,809 per 10 g, and Reuters had spot gold at $3,879.71 an ounce on Friday afternoon in New York, down about 4.5% for the week, its worst week since early June.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: the per-gram ceiling on fresh loans is now ₹439 below the 7–11 Nov level at 85%, and this one runs through Monday. Spot steadied on Friday, so the next AGLOC period will tell whether the cuts are over.
Sources: [AGLOC](https://www.agloc.org/) · [IBJA](https://ibjarates.com/) · [Mint (Reuters)](https://www.livemint.com/market/ratehike-bets-keep-gold-on-track-for-worst-week-in-four-months-11794598496469.html)

### October retail inflation at 6.09% breaks RBI's upper band
<!-- story: id=india-cpi-inflation-2026 -->
<!-- tags: topics=macro,rates; category=macro -->
MoSPI's release puts October CPI inflation at 6.09% (provisional), up from 5.54% in September, with food inflation at 8.94%; rural inflation was 6.63% and urban 5.41%. BusinessLine says it breaches RBI's 6% upper tolerance limit for the first time in two years, and economists expect a rate hike next month.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: another repo hike would feed into bank-loan and CP pricing; bank term loans alone were 39.69% of Muthoot Fincorp's borrowings in March 2026, per BusinessLine's reading of the DRHP. On Friday the 10-year G-sec yield rose six basis points to 7.31%, per Business Standard, and Manappuram's management said on its call that borrowing costs could rise 25–30 bp.
Sources: [MoSPI: CPI October 2026](https://www.mospi.gov.in/uploads/latestReleases/latest_release_1794478693814_7c90e80f-9e64-4aa4-b44a-1dc727b0fed9_Press_Release_of_CPI_for_October_2026_.pdf) · [BusinessLine](https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/markets/sensex-nifty50-today-stock-market-highlights-13-november-2026/article71693403.ece) · [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/economy/news/rupee-weakens-to-94-25-10-year-bond-yield-rises-as-crude-prices-surge-126111301340_1.html) · [Mint](https://www.livemint.com/economy/cpi-retail-inflation-october-india-rbi-target-food-price-inflation-11794467724485.html) · [BusinessLine: Muthoot Fincorp funding mix](https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/news/muthoot-fincorp-diversifies-funding-to-trim-borrowing-cost/article71695055.ece)

## 🏘️ Sector Deep Dive — Saturday: infrastructure and project finance

No infrastructure-finance filing stood out on Friday, but the funding backdrop for long-tenor lenders moved. The 10-year G-sec yield rose six basis points to 7.31% and the rupee weakened to 94.25, per Business Standard, as Brent traded near $95.76 a barrel (BusinessLine) and October CPI came in at 6.09%. For infrastructure financiers that borrow long, higher term rates widen the gap between the cost of new bonds and older fixed-rate loans; the same move reaches gold lenders more slowly because their loans run under a year.
