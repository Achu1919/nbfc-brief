### Muthoot Fincorp Q2 FY27: profit doubles to ₹779 crore, Stage III at 1.07%
<!-- story: id=mfl-q2fy27-results; continues=mfl-ipo-2026; milestone=results -->
<!-- tags: companies=muthoot-fincorp; topics=results,asset-quality; category=muthoot-fincorp -->
<!-- event: id=evt-mfl-results-2026-11-14; status=done; outcome_summary=Board approved unaudited standalone Q2 FY27 results (PAT ₹779.41 crore, Stage III 1.07%, CRAR 19.38%) and recommended CCCPS conversion and reappointment of the three managing directors; outcome_date=2026-11-14; outcome_card=2026-11-16-news-1; outcome_source=https://www.bseindia.com/xml-data/corpfiling/AttachLive/1b013a70-8ca6-4aff-ae25-9ef12e008024.pdf; outcome_story=mfl-q2fy27-results -->
Muthoot Fincorp's board approved its unaudited standalone results on Saturday 14 Nov and filed them with BSE that evening: Q2 FY27 profit was ₹779.41 crore against ₹388.31 crore a year earlier and ₹705.48 crore in Q1, on total income of ₹3,408.34 crore (up 70.7% year on year). Loans stood at ₹54,685.50 crore on 30 September, up 19.3% from 31 March, with Stage III at 1.07% of gross loans, net Stage III at 0.63%, provision coverage at 42.01% and capital adequacy at 19.38%.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: the gross Stage III ratio sits well below the latest standalone prints of Manappuram (1.8%) and Muthoot Finance (3.87%), while impairment charges rose to ₹183.00 crore from ₹106.21 crore a year ago and ₹93.81 crore in Q1, and finance costs rose 68.7% to ₹1,161.53 crore. The same board meeting recommended converting 40 crore compulsorily convertible cumulative preference shares (CCCPS) into equity and reappointing Thomas John Muthoot, Thomas George Muthoot and Thomas Muthoot for five years from 1 February 2027, all subject to shareholder approval.

### RBI deputy governor rules out easing the 50% FOIR cap for microfinance
<!-- story: id=rbi-microfinance-foir-2026; continues=nbfc-rural-stress-2026 -->
<!-- tags: regulators=rbi; topics=microfinance,policy; category=regulation -->
In a speech at an MFIN event in Mumbai on 13 Nov, published by RBI on 15 Nov, Deputy Governor Shirish Chandra Murmu turned down the industry's request to count only the interest part of some loans in the 50% repayment-to-income (FOIR) cap, saying it would "tinker with the sanctity of the limit and understate household leverage". He said RBI would review the ₹3 lakh household-income threshold, flagged rising microfinance lending rates and ticket sizes, and said credit bureaus have been told to move microfinance loans out of the consumer segment.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: the cap governs Muthoot Microfin's book and any microfinance-type lending in the group, so there is no relief on household indebtedness limits. The bureau clean-up means microfinance loans reported under the consumer segment will start showing up in microfinance FOIR checks.
Sources: [RBI speech, 13 Nov 2026](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_SpeechesView.aspx?Id=1590) · [Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/industry/banking/finance/rbi-pushes-back-on-easing-microfinance-foir-flags-rising-rates-and-debt-risks/articleshow/135856447.cms) · [BusinessLine](https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/money-and-banking/mfis-should-lend-responsibly-as-climate-risks-could-impact-borrowers-rbi-deputy-governor/article71705221.ece)

### Sammaan Capital to enter gold loans in 16 branches this quarter
<!-- story: id=sammaan-capital-gold-loan-2026 -->
<!-- tags: companies=sammaan-capital; topics=gold-loan; category=deals -->
IHC-backed Sammaan Capital will soft-launch gold loans in about 16 branches in Q3 FY27 and another 30 in Q4, with an indicative ticket of about ₹2 lakh, yields of 11–13%, tenures up to three years and LTV up to 85% below ₹2.5 lakh, its management said on the Q2 call. ## 🏘️ Sector Deep Dive — Monday: Microfinance & SHG lending

**Where the sector stands.** Sa-Dhan's CEO Jiji Mammen says the microfinance portfolio fell to ₹3.3 lakh crore from ₹4.4 lakh crore before growth turned in March 2026 after about eight quarters of contraction, and he expects 10–15% growth this year, mostly in the second half ([Financial Express](https://www.financialexpress.com/money/mutual-funds-we-are-piloting-mutual-fund-distribution-through-mfis-sa-dhan-ceo-4378955/)). Sa-Dhan and AMFI are piloting mutual-fund distribution through MFIs in 10 states.

**What RBI said.** Deputy Governor Murmu's 13 Nov speech kept the two guardrails, the ₹3 lakh income ceiling (under review) and the 50% FOIR cap (not to be eased), and cited a study finding the average borrower spends 18.7% of monthly household income on repayments ([RBI](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_SpeechesView.aspx?Id=1590)). He named three problems, over-indebtedness, high rates and harsh recovery, and said lending rates are moving up across all types of lenders, partly on higher credit costs. Credit bureaus have been told to disable microfinance reporting in the consumer segment and migrate those records, and the bureaus have built Grameen Credit Score models for rural borrowers.

**What it means for Muthoot Fincorp's group.** Muthoot Microfin, the group's listed microfinance arm, works under the same caps; its board meets on 19 Nov on audited half-year accounts for Muthoot Fincorp's restated consolidated statements ([NSE](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/muthootmicrofin_13112026182612_PriorintimationBoardMeeting19112026SD.pdf)). The bureau migration matters most for borrowers who hold both a microfinance loan and a small gold loan, the overlap the gold lenders' own Stage 2 data has started to show.
