- **Muthoot Fincorp's Q2 profit doubled, and its Stage III ratio is low but up on the quarter.** Muthoot Fincorp's board approved standalone Q2 FY27 results on Sat 14 Nov and filed them on BSE: profit ₹779.41 cr (₹705.48 cr in Q1), loans ₹54,685.50 cr (+19.3% since March), Stage III 1.07%, net Stage III 0.63% and CRAR 19.38% ([BSE filing](https://www.bseindia.com/xml-data/corpfiling/AttachLive/1b013a70-8ca6-4aff-ae25-9ef12e008024.pdf)). ET puts year-ago profit at ₹388 cr and year-ago GNPA at 1.41%, with provisions up to ₹183 cr from ₹106 cr ([Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/industry/banking/finance/ipo-bound-muthoot-fincorp-doubles-q2-profit-to-rs-779-crore-bucks-trend-of-rising-bad-loans/articleshow/135851622.cms)). CARE's October rationale had standalone GNPA at 0.77% on 30 June, so Stage III rose about 30 bps in the quarter ([CARE Ratings](https://www.careratings.com/upload/CompanyFiles/PR/202610121058_Muthoot_Fincorp_Limited.pdf)). ## 🏛️ Regulatory & Policy Watch
<!-- stories: ids=rbi-pe-nbfc-control-curbs-2026,india-cpi-inflation-2026,gold-risk-weight-review-2026,reg-rbi-rbi-dpss-2026-27-476,rbi-nbfc-faq-update-2026-11,reg-rbi-press-release-2026-2027-1471,rbi-mobikwik-penalty-2026-11,reg-sebi-ho-38-14-18-2026-mirsd-pod2-i-25560-2026,reg-rbi-press-release-2026-2027-1447,reg-sebi-ho-17-11-22-1-2026-ddhs-pod1 -->

RBI issued nothing addressed to NBFCs this week. Its notifications feed ends with the 11 Nov Domestic Money Transfer Directions for banks and payment operators ([RBI notifications](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx)). What mattered came from a press report and a price index.

**1. RBI is reportedly drafting curbs on private equity control of NBFCs (Reuters, 12 Nov).** Two people told Reuters that RBI wants to stop one private equity firm holding a controlling stake in more than one NBFC, with holdings of 15% or less treated as financial investments under lighter scrutiny. A draft would go out for consultation first, and the rules could come early next year; RBI did not comment ([Mint/Reuters](https://www.livemint.com/industry/banking/india-plans-curbs-on-private-equity-ownership-of-nbfcs-report-11794472084478.html), [ET/Reuters](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/news/economy/finance/rbi-plans-curbs-on-private-equity-firms-controlling-stakes-in-multiple-non-bank-lenders-report/articleshow/135763029.cms)).
- *Direct impact:* none until RBI publishes a draft.
- *Second order:* it came out the same week IIFL agreed to sell about 16% to Blackstone and Mint listed Bain's 18% of Manappuram among recent foreign stakes ([Mint](https://www.livemint.com/industry/banking/from-banks-to-nbfcs-foreign-capital-chases-india-s-credit-growth-11794389152871.html)). Minority stakes of 15% or less stay easy. Control deals get harder for funds that already run a lender.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: none until a draft appears. Minority stakes of 15% or less, the usual size for IPO investors, would sit outside the reported curbs, and RBI's existing prior-approval rule for control or 26%-plus stakes applies either way.

**2. October CPI at 6.09% puts a December hike in play.** MoSPI's provisional print is 6.09% against 5.54% in September, with food inflation at 8.94%, rural 6.63% and urban 5.41% ([MoSPI](https://www.mospi.gov.in/uploads/latestReleases/latest_release_1794478693814_7c90e80f-9e64-4aa4-b44a-1dc727b0fed9_Press_Release_of_CPI_for_October_2026_.pdf)). BusinessLine reports that market players expect a 25 bp hike to 5.75% after October's increase to 5.50% ([BusinessLine](https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/money-and-banking/g-sec-yields-harden-as-oct-inflation-breaches-6-mark-us-yields-rise/article71698427.ece)).
- *Direct impact:* repo-linked bank lines and CP reprice if RBI moves on 4 Dec.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: bank term loans were 39.69% of its borrowings in March 2026, per BusinessLine's reading of the DRHP ([BusinessLine](https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/news/muthoot-fincorp-diversifies-funding-to-trim-borrowing-cost/article71695055.ece)), and that is the part that reprices first.

**3. RBI's instruments this week were small.** The Domestic Money Transfer Directions, 2026 (RBI/DPSS/2026-27/476, 11 Nov) consolidate rules for banks and payment operators; cash pay-outs stay capped at ₹10,000 a transaction ([RBI](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=13752&Mode=0)). RBI refreshed its NBFC FAQ page ("Updated as on November 11, 2026") ([RBI FAQs](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/FAQView.aspx?Id=92)), cancelled Gandhinagar Leasing and Finance's registration ([RBI](https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx?prid=63949)) and fined One Mobikwik Systems ₹9 lakh, partly for late fraud reporting ([RBI](https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx?prid=63973)). SEBI aligned intermediaries' FATCA/CRS reporting with the Income-Tax Rules, 2026 ([SEBI](https://www.sebi.gov.in/legal/circulars/nov-2026/reporting-requirement-under-foreign-account-tax-compliance-act-fatca-and-common-reporting-standard-crs-revised-through-income-tax-act-2025-read-with-income-tax-rules-2026_105705.html)).
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: no new requirement. The FAQ refresh was not compared line by line with the earlier version, so it is treated here as a restatement unless RBI flags a change.

**4. CII asked for a review of the 100% risk weight on gold loans.** The ask went to the Finance Ministry with easier ECB conditions for NBFCs ([CNBC-TV18](https://www.cnbctv18.com/economy/cii-recommends-regulatory-relaxations-nbfcs-reform-agenda-to-finance-ministry20030393-20030393.htm)). It's an industry proposal; neither RBI nor the government has responded.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: a lower risk weight would raise its capital adequacy for the same gold book; no regulator has proposed one.

**5. Still open.** Comments on RBI's draft PSL Directions are due on 26 Nov ([RBI](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx?prid=63925)), and SEBI's Credit Risk-o-Meter for debt offer documents starts on 21 Nov ([SEBI](https://www.sebi.gov.in/legal/circulars/oct-2026/introduction-of-credit-risk-o-meter-as-an-additional-disclosure-mechanism-for-debt-securities_105081.html)). The revolving-credit and Interest Rate Directions drafts are still not final ([RBI drafts](https://rbi.org.in/Scripts/DraftNotificationsGuildelines.aspx)).
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: the Risk-o-Meter would apply to any public NCD tranche it files after 21 Nov under its ₹4,000 cr shelf.

## 💳 Credit & Lending Trends
<!-- stories: ids=gold-loan-asset-quality-2026,mfl-ipo-2026,iifl-finance-blackstone-raise-2026 -->

- **Bank credit picked up into Diwali.** Scheduled commercial bank credit grew 19.5% year on year in the fortnight to 31 Oct, against deposits at 16.9%; credit rose 1.9% in the fortnight alone ([RBI WSS](https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx?prid=63972)). The credit-deposit gap widened to about 2.5 percentage points on the all-scheduled-banks series, per PTI ([ETBFSI](https://bfsi.economictimes.indiatimes.com/articles/bank-credit-growth-picks-up-to-19-26-pc-in-fortnight-ended-oct-31-deposits-up-16-76-pc-rbi/135803840)). ## 💡 Analyst's Take
<!-- stories: ids=gold-loan-asset-quality-2026,mfl-q2fy27-results,iifl-finance-blackstone-raise-2026,rbi-pe-nbfc-control-curbs-2026 -->

The week's surface story is that gold lenders had a bad week: gold fell, the Stage 2 numbers came out, and Manappuram dropped almost 5% on a record profit. Underneath, the market is separating two kinds of gold lender. Diversified lenders with access to equity (Bajaj, IIFL, Godrej Capital, and now Aditya Birla and Sammaan opening gold branches) are being paid to grow. The pure gold lenders are being priced on how well their borrowers adjust to monthly payments. Last week's macro tailwind lasted five sessions. CPI at 6.09% and a hot US print took half of it back, and a December hike is now the base case.

Some of the stress is mechanical. A bullet borrower who misses a monthly interest date lands in Stage 2 even with 20 points of LTV cushion. Bajaj's cure rate, about half of its build back in Stage 1 within a quarter, suggests much of it will reverse. The contrarian concern runs the other way. The bureau data show loan counts down 15% while value rose 12%, so tickets are getting bigger, and Macquarie says some lenders lend to borrowers already overdue elsewhere. Bigger tickets, falling gold and a new payment habit make a worse mix than any one of them alone. Muthoot Fincorp's 1.07% Stage III headline will read well against Manappuram's 1.80%. The question analysts will ask is what its Stage 2 did, and the filing's ratio annexure doesn't say.
