### RBI deputy governor: microfinance FOIR cap stays at 50%, income limit under review
<!-- story: id=rbi-microfinance-foir-2026; dup -->
This is a speech, not a circular, so no rule changes. Deputy Governor Murmu said on 13 Nov that excluding the principal of some loans from the 50% FOIR calculation "would defeat this purpose", that the ₹3 lakh household-income ceiling will be reviewed, and that credit bureaus have been told to move microfinance records out of the consumer segment.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: the FOIR rule and the ₹3 lakh ceiling are unchanged for Muthoot Microfin and any group microfinance lending; a revised income threshold would come only through an amendment to the microfinance Directions.
Sources: [RBI speech, 13 Nov 2026](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_SpeechesView.aspx?Id=1590) · [Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/industry/banking/finance/rbi-pushes-back-on-easing-microfinance-foir-flags-rising-rates-and-debt-risks/articleshow/135856447.cms)
