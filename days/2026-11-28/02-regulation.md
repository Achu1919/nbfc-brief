**Date:** Saturday, 28 November 2026 (IST)

## 📖 The standing rulebook: the LTV bands, unchanged; AGLOC's new block runs 28-30 November
<!-- story: id=reg-rbi-rbi-2025-26-47 -->
The gold-loan LTV bands this week's reporting rests on are the standing ones: **85% for loans up to ₹2.5 lakh, 80% between ₹2.5 and 5 lakh, 75% above ₹5 lakh — effective 1 April 2026 — with LTV maintained through the loan tenure and a breach treated as default even without a missed payment.** **No RBI instrument dated 26, 27 or 28 November changes them, and nothing on the 27 November block touches any other rule applying to Muthoot Fincorp's lending business.** What moved is the published ceiling: **AGLOC's new block — headed 30 November 2026 and dated 28-30 November on its own table, so it carries this weekend and Monday rather than Monday alone — puts the bands at ₹9,833 at 85%, ₹9,255 at 80% and ₹8,676 at 75%, on a jewellery reference of ₹11,569** — ₹285, ₹268 and ₹251 above the 27 November block, the reference up ₹336. The reference is a mechanical re-strike, not new discovery: **₹11,569 is IBJA's 27 November PM 916 rate ÷ 10 exactly**, and **the bands move together because they are fixed percentages of the one reference — one observation, not three**.

Beside the bands sits one representation, not a rule: **ET reported on 26 November that banks and gold-loan companies such as Muthoot and Manappuram have informed the RBI that, as a prudential measure, they have lowered the LTV on fresh loans to an average of 60–65% from about 75%.** ET names "Muthoot" without saying which entity — Muthoot Finance is the listed company; Muthoot Fincorp is not named — and a representation to the RBI is not a rule change: **the RBI has not relaxed or cut anything; the bands stand, and writing below them is prudential discretion, not a requirement.**

Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: these bands and this reference govern the ceiling the company lends under — ₹9,833 at the 85% band for the block running 28-30 November, ₹285 above the previous block; nothing about the company's own book is inferred from either.
Sources: [AGLOC, block headed 30 Nov 2026, dated 28-30 Nov](https://www.agloc.org/) · [IBJA](https://ibjarates.com/) · [Financial Express, 23 Nov 2026 — the standing bands](https://www.financialexpress.com/market/gold-loan-lenders-face-collateral-pressure-as-prices-slide-4391109/) · [Economic Times, 26 Nov 05:30 IST](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/industry/banking/finance/banking/gold-crash-triggers-auction-notices-as-ltv-breaches-hit-lenders/articleshow/136172114.cms)
