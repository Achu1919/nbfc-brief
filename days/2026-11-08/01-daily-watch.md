# 📊 NBFC Daily Watch — Sunday, November 8, 2026

Morning edition for Sunday, 8 November 2026 (IST). Covers 6–7 Nov and overnight. Diwali Muhurat trading runs on NSE and BSE this afternoon (2:20–3:30 PM IST); the last full session was Friday, 6 Nov.

## 🔴 URGENT / High Impact

### Muhurat trading today after the Nifty's best week in this run: +8.58%
<!-- tags: topics=markets; category=urgent -->
NSE's circular confirms a special Diwali Muhurat session on Sunday, 8 Nov, with continuous trading from 14:20 to 15:30 IST, and every trade carries settlement obligations. The session follows a week in which the Nifty 50 rose 8.58% (23,053.10 on 30 Oct to 25,030.35 on 6 Nov) and Nifty Financial Services rose 10.41% (24,712.20 to 27,283.95), per NSE index close files.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: the session is the last equity print before the scheduled Tranche I NCD allotment, and a financials index up 10% in a week forms the backdrop for the NCD listing and for comparisons with listed gold lenders.
Source: [NSE circular 149/2026](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/content/circulars/CMTR76941.pdf)

## 🏢 Muthoot Fincorp in the market

### ICRA finalises Muthoot Fincorp's Delta 3.0 PTC ratings; Delta 5 gets provisional AA+(SO)
<!-- tags: companies=muthoot-fincorp; topics=rating,funding; category=muthoot-fincorp -->
On 6 Nov ICRA confirmed as final its ratings on PTCs issued by Delta 3.0, backed by Muthoot Fincorp's unsecured QR code-based merchant loan receivables. On 4 Nov it assigned provisional [ICRA]AA+(SO) to PTC Series A1 and provisional [ICRA]AA-(SO) to the equity PTC of Delta 5, backed by a similar merchant-loan pool.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: another Delta transaction extends securitisation funding for the unsecured QR merchant-loan book; the Delta 5 ratings remain provisional until confirmed.
Source: [ICRA — Delta 3.0 final](https://www.icra.in/Rationale/ShowRationaleReport?Id=146789)

## 📈 Market & Stock Summary

### Week in review: gold-loan peers lead a sharp financials rally
<!-- tags: companies=muthoot-finance,manappuram,iifl-finance,shriram-finance,bajaj-finance,muthoot-microfin; topics=markets; category=markets -->
From the 30 Oct close to the 6 Nov close on NSE, Muthoot Finance rose 19.11% to ₹2,869.00, IIFL Finance 18.59% to ₹625.75, Shriram Finance 16.76% to ₹961.15, Bajaj Finance 15.83% to ₹1,087.75, Muthoot Microfin 14.12% to ₹153.92 and Manappuram 12.60% to ₹325.40. All six beat the Nifty 50's 8.58% weekly gain.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: listed gold lenders re-rated sharply in a single week despite Muthoot Finance's Q2 Stage 3 increase; peer valuations are the comparison set for Muthoot Fincorp's proposed ₹3,000 crore IPO.
Source: [NSE bhavcopy 6 Nov](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/products/content/sec_bhavdata_full_06112026.csv)

### Spot gold slips 0.3% on Friday but posts its best week since August
<!-- tags: topics=gold-price,markets; category=markets -->
Reuters, via Economic Times, reported spot gold down 0.3% at $4,074.36 an ounce at 18:54 GMT on 6 Nov after stronger-than-expected US payrolls, but up 5.2% for the week, its best week since August. Gold had touched a one-year low of $3,869.85 on 30 Oct.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: a 5% weekly rebound in dollar gold raises collateral values for gold lenders, though IBJA's 999 rate fell 1.86% on 6 Nov and AGLOC's 7–9 Nov ceilings reflect that lower Friday rate.
Source: [Economic Times / Reuters](https://m.economictimes.com/markets/commodities/news/gold-drops-as-strong-us-payrolls-boost-dollar-yields/amp_articleshow/135626183.cms)

## 💰 Financial Results & Performance

### Muthoot Capital Services Q2: PAT ₹4.40 crore; GNPA rises to 4.81% from 3.94% in June
<!-- tags: companies=muthoot-capital; topics=results,asset-quality,vehicle-finance; category=results -->
Muthoot Capital Services, a separate listed Muthoot Pappachan Group company (not Muthoot Fincorp, not Muthoot Finance), reported Q2 FY27 PAT of ₹440.25 lakh, against ₹282.72 lakh a year earlier and ₹812.21 lakh in Q1. GNPA including interest accrual was 4.81% of advances, against 3.94% in June and 6.46% a year earlier; its shares closed 4.28% lower at ₹195.03 on 6 Nov.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: a rising NPA ratio at a group-name company may draw attention to the separation between Muthoot Pappachan Group entities as Muthoot Fincorp's NCDs list.
Source: [NSE results filing](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/content/debt/WDM/MUTHOOTCAPS_05112026211829_Final.pdf)

## 📉 NPA & Asset Quality

### Aadhar Housing Finance: Stage 2 up 29 bps to about 3.6% in Q2
<!-- tags: companies=aadhar-housing-finance; topics=asset-quality,housing-finance; category=npa -->
ETBFSI Research reports Aadhar Housing Finance's Stage 2 rose about 29 bps sequentially to around 3.6% in the September quarter, the second straight quarter of deterioration. Gross Stage 3 rose 4 bps to 1.35%, while credit costs eased to about 30 bps from 41 bps.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: early-bucket slippage is rising in affordable housing and LAP, segments Muthoot Fincorp's DRHP lists for growth beyond gold.
Source: [ETBFSI](https://bfsi.economictimes.indiatimes.com/articles/aadhar-housing-finance-sees-second-straight-quarter-of-asset-quality-deterioration/135624585)

## 🏘️ Sector Deep Dive — Sunday: Weekly preview + sector health check

### The week ahead: allotment, listing, a peer earnings call and the Reg 52 deadline
<!-- tags: companies=muthoot-fincorp,manappuram; topics=ncd,results,markets; category=sector -->
Muhurat trading is today. Muthoot Fincorp's Tranche I allotment and BSE listing fall on Monday and Tuesday, and Manappuram Finance holds its Q2 FY27 earnings call on Thursday 12 Nov at 5 PM IST. Listed-debt issuers' Q2 results under SEBI LODR Regulation 52 are due by 14 Nov.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: Manappuram's call comes two days before the Reg 52 deadline, so its Stage 2 and Stage 3 commentary under the April gold-loan rules is the nearest peer benchmark for Muthoot Fincorp's Q2 disclosure.
Source: [NSE — Manappuram earnings call notice](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/MANAPPURAM_05112026222522_prior_intimation_concall_SD.pdf)

### Health check: strong growth, rising early-bucket stress
<!-- tags: companies=finkurve; topics=gold-loan,asset-quality,results; category=sector -->
The sector numbers point in two directions. Small gold lender Finkurve (Arvog) reported Q2 FY27 AUM up 95.4% YoY to ₹1,312 crore with GNPA at 2.63%. Elsewhere, Aadhar Housing's Stage 2 and Muthoot Capital Services' GNPA both rose quarter on quarter, and RBI's 6 Nov data (covered yesterday) had NBFC gold-jewellery loans up 66.1% YoY.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: fast gold-book growth across the sector, small lenders included, alongside early-stage slippage in adjacent products, is the setting for Muthoot Fincorp's Q2 results.
Source: [NSE — Finkurve press release](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/FINKURVE_05112026195145_Press_release.pdf)
