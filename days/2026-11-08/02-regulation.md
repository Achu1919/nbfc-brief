## 🔔 New Regulatory Updates Today

✅ No new RBI, SEBI, IBBI, NHB or FIU-IND instrument for NBFCs since Friday evening. Two items worth logging are below.

### RBI issues NBFC Certificate of Registration to MobiKwik's lending arm
<!-- tags: regulators=rbi; topics=digital-lending,policy; companies=mobikwik -->
One MobiKwik Systems disclosed on 6 Nov that RBI has issued a Certificate of Registration under the RBI Act to its wholly owned subsidiary, MobiKwik Financial Services Pvt Ltd, to operate as an NBFC. The company says lending will start in three to six months, first through co-lending with banks and NBFCs.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: no rule change; the registration adds another regulated fintech-owned lender in small-ticket Tier 2 and 3 credit.
Reference: company disclosure under SEBI LODR Reg 30/51 (no RBI circular) · Addressed to: stock exchanges · Issued: 6 Nov 2026
Source: [NSE filing](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/MOBIKWIK_06112026141941_Intimation_about_granting_of_NBFC_License.pdf)

### Watchlist sweep: nothing new for gold-loan NBFCs on 6–7 Nov
RBI's notifications feed ends with the 5 Nov Master Directions on cyber resilience for non-bank payment system operators. Its press releases end with routine 6 Nov items: the September NBFC sectoral credit release (covered yesterday), a 4-day VRR auction for 9 Nov, a T-bill auction, the FLA census, and a Section 35A direction on one co-operative bank. Nothing on the Master Directions page has an NBFC "updated as on" date in November. SEBI's newest circular is still 5 Nov, and its 6 Nov items are the settlement-regulations commencement (covered yesterday) plus recovery and adjudication orders. The IBBI site shows a last update of 31 Oct, NHB's newest notice (4 Nov) is a broker-empanelment waiver, and FIU-IND's homepage gave no dated notice to parse.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: none today. The open regulatory dates for listed-debt NBFCs remain the 21 Nov Credit Risk-o-Meter start and the 26 Nov PSL comment window.
