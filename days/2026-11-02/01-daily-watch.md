## 🔴 URGENT / High Impact

**1. Muthoot Finance explains its Stage 3 jump: short-tenure and monthly-EMI gold loans are tripping into overdue buckets**
On its Q2 call, Muthoot Finance said Stage 3 rose to ₹7,267 crore (3.87% of loan assets) from ₹2,977 crore a year ago, and Stage 2 (31–89 days) jumped to ₹23,467 crore from ₹534 crore. MD Alexander George Muthoot tied it to the shorter-tenure and interest-servicing schemes that followed RBI's gold-loan directions (effective 1 April 2026). Management said about ₹3,000 crore of the Stage 3 pool is second or third accounts of borrowers whose other loans are still in Stage 1 or 2, clubbed together because it classifies borrower-wise. It quoted LTVs of 53% on Stage 3 and 72% on Stage 2, and said auction is the last resort. The article also notes Shriram Finance and Bajaj Finance flagged higher gold-loan slippages after the same shift. ([Financial Express](https://www.financialexpress.com/business/news/short-tenure-loans-monthly-emis-drive-up-gold-loan-npas-muthoot-finance-md/4374640/))
> **Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp:** Muthoot Fincorp is subject to the same April 2026 gold-loan directions, so analysts are likely to read its Q2 Stage 2 and Stage 3 figures against this disclosure.

**2. Bank lending to NBFCs up 41.8% year on year in September**
Bank credit to NBFCs reached ₹22.53 lakh crore in September, up 41.8% from a year earlier (3.7% growth a year ago). NBFCs now make up about 35% of bank lending to services (₹64.9 lakh crore, +28.3%). Lending to HFCs grew 19.7%; overall bank credit grew 19.4% to ₹229.95 lakh crore. ETBFSI raises the question of whether some of this is circular credit growth. ([ETBFSI](https://bfsi.economictimes.indiatimes.com/articles/bank-lending-to-nbfcs-surges-42-raising-questions-over-circular-credit-growth/135441224); underlying data: [RBI sectoral deployment, Sept 2026](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx?prid=63891))
> **Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp:** Bank lines are a funding source for NBFCs including Muthoot Fincorp; RBI raised risk weights on bank lending to NBFCs after a similar surge in November 2023 ([RBI/2023-24/85](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=12567&Mode=0)), so any repeat would affect the cost of that funding across the sector.

**3. Nomura's bear case: weak monsoons could hit rural NBFC books**
The south-west monsoon ended 13% short; the north-east monsoon was 54% below normal over 1–24 October, with South Peninsula 31% short; rabi sowing is down 37% year on year. Nomura's bear case assumes a 7.25% risk-free rate (vs 6.25%) and halved insurance fee income from FY28. Its preferred names are Piramal, Tata Capital, Aadhar Housing and Bajaj Finance; it notes CreditAccess Grameen trades below previous-crisis multiples. ([ETBFSI](https://bfsi.economictimes.indiatimes.com/articles/nbfcs-brace-for-rural-loan-stress-as-drought-and-weak-monsoon-hit-farm-incomes/135441225))
> **Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp:** Muthoot Fincorp is headquartered in Kerala and lends heavily in south India, so a weak north-east monsoon over the South Peninsula touches the region where much of its gold-loan book sits.

## 🏭 Sector Deep Dive — Monday: Microfinance & SHG lending

**Where the sector stands.** Sa-Dhan's Bharat Microfinance Report says the industry book shrank 11% in FY26 to ₹3.34 trillion, PAR 180+ rose to 17.04% from 10.67%, and active accounts fell 21% to 10.40 crore. The report sees early signs of recovery, but legacy bad loans still weigh ([Mint](https://www.livemint.com/news/microfinance-shows-early-recovery-signs-but-legacy-bad-loans-weigh-on-sector-11791450920201.html)).

**The guardrails.** MFIN's rules cap a borrower at three lenders, total MFI debt within ₹2 lakh, and no fresh loans to anyone 60+ days past due. Industry leaders are pushing diversification into secured and individual loans ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/specials/bs-events/microfinance-lenders-eye-diversification-as-sector-recovers-industry-leaders-126102600830_1.html)).

**The guarantee gap.** CGFMU ended on 31 March 2026, and banks want it extended. IDFC First's ₹6,825 crore MFI book is 97% covered and AU SFB's is 99% covered, which shows how much lenders leaned on it ([Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/industry/banking/finance/banking/banks-seek-microloan-guarantee-extension/articleshow/135435172.cms)).

**The new risk.** The weak north-east monsoon and 37% lower rabi sowing hit exactly the rural borrowers MFIs serve; Nomura notes CreditAccess Grameen trades below previous-crisis multiples ([ETBFSI](https://bfsi.economictimes.indiatimes.com/articles/nbfcs-brace-for-rural-loan-stress-as-drought-and-weak-monsoon-hit-farm-incomes/135441225)).

**Muthoot Microfin.** Muthoot Microfin, Muthoot Fincorp's listed microfinance subsidiary, reported Q2 FY27 AUM of ₹15,323 crore (+22%), disbursements of ₹2,900 crore (+28%), collection efficiency of 98.11% and cost of funds of 9.93%. Its JLG to non-JLG mix moved to 69:31 from 83:17 in March, and it disbursed ₹453 crore of gold loans under a referral and co-lending arrangement with Muthoot Fincorp. These are provisional, unaudited business-update figures ([CNBC-TV18](https://www.cnbctv18.com/market/muthoot-microfin-share-price-q2-biz-update-aum-growth-gold-loans-sme-jlg-mix-cost-of-funds-drop-20007277.htm)).

> **Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp:** Muthoot Microfin's results are consolidated into Muthoot Fincorp's, so south-India drought stress on joint-liability loans, with CGFMU cover lapsed, would show up in the group's consolidated credit costs.
