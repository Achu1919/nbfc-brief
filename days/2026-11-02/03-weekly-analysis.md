# 📋 NBFC Weekly Analysis: Week of 26 October – 1 November 2026

*Published Monday, 2 November 2026 (IST). Covers trading sessions Mon 26 to Fri 30 October, plus Muthoot Finance's Q2 on Saturday 31 October and weekend news. Muthoot Fincorp Ltd is a Middle Layer NBFC with unlisted equity and listed NCDs. Muthoot Finance Ltd is a separate, listed company; none of its numbers are Muthoot Fincorp's.*

## Executive Summary

- **The gold-loan stress story got its biggest data point yet, and it came from a peer.** Muthoot Finance (not Muthoot Fincorp) reported standalone Stage 3 of 3.87%, up from 2.28% in June, and Stage 2 of ₹23,467 cr against ₹534 cr a year ago. Its MD blamed the short-tenure and monthly-servicing schemes that followed RBI's 1 April 2026 gold-lending directions, and said about ₹3,000 cr of Stage 3 is borrower-wise clubbing of accounts that are otherwise current ([ET](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/markets/stocks/earnings/muthoot-finance-q2-cons-profit-jumps-36-yoy-to-rs-3278-crore/articleshow/135401237.cms), [Financial Express](https://www.financialexpress.com/business/news/short-tenure-loans-monthly-emis-drive-up-gold-loan-npas-muthoot-finance-md/4374640/)). Shriram, Bajaj Finance and Piramal had already flagged the same thing.
- **Gold fell again, hard.** Spot gold lost 3.04% on Friday alone to $3,899.33, its first close under $3,900 since mid-July, and ended the week about 3% lower. MCX December gold finished Friday's session at ₹1,38,419/10g ([CNBC-TV18](https://www.cnbctv18.com/market/commodities/gold-silver-prices-crash-mcx-gold-plunges-to-rs-1-lakh-silver-falls-to-rs-1-lakh-per-kg-20023794.htm), [ET](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/commoditysummary/symbol-GOLD.cms)). Listed gold lenders gave back most of Thursday's rally on Friday.
- **Bond yields finally broke their losing run, but lenders are still raising rates.** The 10-year G-sec fell about 6 bps on the week to 7.414%, ending a 10-week selloff ([ET](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/markets/bonds/india-bonds-buckle-for-fourth-straight-month-on-oils-climb/articleshow/135361502.cms)). Even so, Tata Capital, PNB Housing and LIC Housing all announced lending-rate increases from 1 November, and ABCL, Chola and Five-Star each put a number on rising funding costs.
- **Banks are pouring money into NBFCs.** Bank credit to NBFCs was up 41.8% year on year in September to ₹22.53 lakh cr ([ETBFSI](https://bfsi.economictimes.indiatimes.com/articles/bank-lending-to-nbfcs-surges-42-raising-questions-over-circular-credit-growth/135441224)). That helps funding now, but it is the kind of number that has drawn RBI risk-weight action before (November 2023).
- **Muthoot Fincorp's week: Tranche I bids went from 0.55× to 1.86×.** About ₹372 cr was bid against the ₹500 cr full issue size (₹200 cr base + ₹300 cr green shoe), with bidding closing Thu 5 Nov ([Chittorgarh](https://www.chittorgarh.com/bond/muthoot-fincorp-tranche-i-ncd-october-2026/399/)). ## 🏛️ Regulatory & Policy Watch

A quiet week for new rules. Nothing RBI issued changes how an NBFC lends, prices or provisions. The work this week was in the sanctions file and in reading what the April gold-loan directions are doing to peer books.

**1. Three UAPA Section 51A circulars addressed to NBFCs (27 and 30 Oct).** `RBI/2026-27/301` removed 3 entries from the UNSC ISIL & Al-Qaida list, `/302` amended 3 entries on the same list, and `/303` amended 5 entries on the Taliban list. All three refer to Chapter IX of the KYC Directions, 2025 ([RBI /301](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=13747&Mode=0), [RBI /302](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=13748&Mode=0), [RBI /303](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=13749&Mode=0)).
- *Direct impact:* same-day screening updates. Amendments matter more than removals, because a new alias or passport number can create a fresh hit on an existing customer.
- *Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp:* compliance work for its AML screening; no direct financial impact.

**2. RBI sectoral credit data for September (30 Oct).** Non-food bank credit +19.1%, services +28.3%, with NBFCs among the "buoyant" segments. Bank loans against gold jewellery slowed versus last year but "remained strong" ([RBI PR 2026-2027/1412](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx?prid=63891)). ETBFSI's read of the same data puts bank credit to NBFCs at +41.8% ([ETBFSI](https://bfsi.economictimes.indiatimes.com/articles/bank-lending-to-nbfcs-surges-42-raising-questions-over-circular-credit-growth/135441224)).
- *Second order:* in November 2023 RBI raised risk weights on bank exposure to NBFCs after a similar run. Nothing has been announced this time, but the 2–4 Dec policy and the next Financial Stability Report are the places a signal would show up.
- *Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp:* bank term loans are one of its funding sources, so any supervisory tightening on bank lending to NBFCs would bear on the cost of renewals.

**3. SEBI and IBBI housekeeping.** SEBI gave debenture trustees until 27 Apr 2027 to ring-fence non-SEBI business ([SEBI](https://www.sebi.gov.in/legal/circulars/oct-2026/extension-of-timeline-for-compliance-with-terms-and-conditions-by-debenture-trustees-dts-for-carrying-out-activities-outside-the-purview-of-sebi_105454.html)), extended digital-accessibility deadlines for its regulated entities to 31 Jan 2027 ([SEBI](https://www.sebi.gov.in/legal/circulars/oct-2026/extension-of-timeline-with-respect-to-compliance-of-digital-accessibility-circulars_105523.html)), and eased FPI KYC review periodicity. IBBI issued two real-estate CIRP circulars, `IBBI/CIRP/109/2026` and `/110/2026` ([IBBI](https://ibbi.gov.in/en/legal-framework/circulars)).
- *Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp:* none directly. Muthoot Fincorp is a debt issuer rather than a SEBI regulated entity, and it participates as a financial creditor in CIRP. The creditor-led CIIRP route under the IBC (Amendment) Act 2026 is still not notified ([NCLT commencement notification](https://nclt.gov.in/sites/default/files/Act%26rules/The%20Insolvency%20and%20Bankruptcy%20Code%20%28Amendment%29%20Act%2C%202026%20Enforment%20Date%20Notification.pdf)).

**4. Not a regulation, but it reads like one: the April gold-loan directions are now visible in Stage 2 and 3.** Muthoot Finance's MD described the 1 April 2026 rules as a learning curve for the company and its customers that will stabilise over a few quarters ([NDTV Profit](https://www.ndtvprofit.com/markets/muthoot-finance-q2-results-profit-rises-27-to-rs-2-977-crore-revises-gold-loan-growth-guidance-for-fy27-12263278)).
- *Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp:* it is subject to the same directions, and its Q2 results (LODR deadline 14 Nov) are likely to be compared with this print, particularly how much of any Stage 3 movement is rule-driven or borrower-wise clubbing versus collection stress.

**5. Still pending.** The revolving-credit draft (6 Aug) and Interest Rate Directions draft (12 Aug) have not been finalised; RBI's latest draft is still 11 Sep ([RBI drafts](https://rbi.org.in/Scripts/DraftNotificationsGuildelines.aspx)). RBI has described the revolving-credit restriction as a clarification of existing rules ([Mint](https://www.livemint.com/industry/banking/rbi-says-nbfc-ban-on-revolving-credit-is-a-clarification-not-a-new-rule-11791378140568.html)). The SEBI Credit Risk-o-Meter goes live on 21 Nov and will apply to debt offer documents filed after that date, including Muthoot Fincorp's ([SEBI](https://www.sebi.gov.in/legal/circulars/oct-2026/introduction-of-credit-risk-o-meter-as-an-additional-disclosure-mechanism-for-debt-securities_105081.html)).

## ⚠️ Asset Quality Deep Dive

**Gold loans: the mechanics are now on the record.** Here is what the week added:
- **Muthoot Finance:** Stage 3 ₹7,267 cr (3.87%), Stage 2 ₹23,467 cr. About ₹3,000 cr of Stage 3 is second or third accounts of borrowers whose other loans are current, pulled in by borrower-wise classification. LTV on Stage 3 is 53% and on Stage 2 is 72%, and auction is "the last resort" ([Financial Express](https://www.financialexpress.com/business/news/short-tenure-loans-monthly-emis-drive-up-gold-loan-npas-muthoot-finance-md/4374640/)). Cofacto's read of the filing puts the quarter's impairment charge at ₹514.92 cr ([Cofacto](https://cofacto.ai/market-news/muthootfin-q2-fy27-results-analysis)).
- **Shriram:** gold-book GS3 3.12% from 2.57% ([VCCircle/Reuters](https://www.vccircle.com/shriramfinance-joins-peers-in-flagging-stress-in-gold-loans)).
- **Piramal's estimate** that 15–23% of FY26 industry gold disbursements (about ₹4.7 lakh cr) went to borrowers already 90+ DPD elsewhere kept circulating ([Moneycontrol](https://www.moneycontrol.com/news/business/commodities/why-are-nbfcs-flagging-stress-in-gold-loans-despite-a-rush-to-expand-14060075.html)).

Two different things are getting lumped together. The first is technical: monthly-servicing products and borrower-wise tagging push loans into Stage 2/3 that are well covered by gold (53% LTV on Stage 3 is a lot of cushion). The second is real: a subset of borrowers were already stretched elsewhere, and a 3% gold fall in a day plus a weak monsoon in the south squeezes exactly those people. The first one fades as customers learn the new servicing rhythm. The second one doesn't.

**Rural and drought stress.** The south-west monsoon ended 13% short, the north-east monsoon was 54% below normal over 1–24 Oct (South Peninsula −31%), and rabi sowing is down 37% YoY ([ETBFSI](https://bfsi.economictimes.indiatimes.com/articles/nbfcs-brace-for-rural-loan-stress-as-drought-and-weak-monsoon-hit-farm-incomes/135441225)). ## 💡 Analyst's Take

The signal this week was Muthoot Finance's Stage 2 going from ₹534 cr to ₹23,467 cr. The 3.87% Stage 3 headline matters less. A move that size in a 31–89 day bucket isn't a credit event; it's a servicing pattern changing under new rules. Management's own detail (53% LTV on Stage 3, ₹3,000 cr of clubbed accounts) says the collateral is fine. The question is whether that Stage 2 pool rolls into Stage 3 over the next two quarters or cures. If it cures, Q2 FY27 will look like a one-time reset. If gold keeps falling and the north-east monsoon failure hits farm cash flow in Tamil Nadu, Kerala and Andhra, the roll rate goes up just when the cushion is shrinking. Both things are happening at once, and they're easy to confuse.

The rate story is quieter but more certain. The 10-year rally got the headlines, but every large lender that reported this week guided to higher funding costs and most repriced their loans from 1 November. The market is pricing a bigger December RBI move through OIS, not a pause. Meanwhile banks lent 41.8% more to NBFCs than a year ago. That money is cheap and plentiful today, and RBI has shown before that it will slow it with risk weights.
