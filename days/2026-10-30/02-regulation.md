## 📅 Regulatory calendar

- **30 Oct 2026:** half-yearly CIMS upload on calamity relief (a NIL return is required if no relief was given).
- **14 Nov 2026:** last date for Q2 FY27 results of listed debt issuers under SEBI LODR Regulation 52.
- **21 Nov 2026:** SEBI Credit Risk-o-Meter disclosure applies to new debt offer documents.
- **31 Dec 2026:** Type I NBFC deregistration / NBFC-AA interoperability window closes.
- **1 Jan 2027:** recovery-agent requirements under the RBC Third Amendment take effect.
- **1 Apr 2027:** Interest Rate Directions take effect, if finalised.
- **15 Jan 2027:** Internal Ombudsman quarterly return for Oct–Dec due ([RBI](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx?Id=13274&Mode=0)).
- **27 Apr 2027:** debenture trustees' deadline to ring-fence non-SEBI business.
- **30 Sep 2027:** last date to reverse unrealised pre-SNFA interest on legacy SNFA stock.
