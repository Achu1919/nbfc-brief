### The auction rulebook the lead story runs under: RBI (Lending Against Gold and Silver Collateral) Directions, 2025
<!-- reg: ref=RBI/2025-26/47 DOR.CRE.REC.26/21.01.023/2025-26; regulator=RBI; issued=2025-06-06; effective=2026-04-01; status=in-force; lifecycle=effective; applies=nbfcs -->
<!-- story: id=reg-rbi-rbi-2025-26-47 -->
The Directions were issued on 6 June 2025 (RBI/2025-26/47 DOR.CRE.REC.26/21.01.023/2025-26) and updated on 29 September 2025 by the (1st Amendment) Directions (RBI/2025-26/84 DOR.CRE.REC.52/21.01.023/2025-26); compliance was required no later than 1 April 2026, and loans sanctioned before a lender adopted the Directions stay under the earlier rules. On tenor, collateral and lending bands: para 15 caps the tenor of bullet-repayment consumption loans at 12 months, renewable under para 11; para 16 caps ornaments at 1 kg of gold (10 kg of silver) and coins at 50 g of gold (500 g of silver) per borrower; para 17 values the collateral at the lower of the average closing price of that purity over the preceding 30 days and the closing price of that purity on the preceding day, as published by IBJA or a SEBI-regulated commodity exchange; para 19 sets the maximum loan-to-value at 85% for loans up to ₹2.5 lakh, 80% above ₹2.5 lakh up to ₹5 lakh, and 75% above ₹5 lakh; and para 20 requires the prescribed LTV to be maintained on an ongoing basis throughout the tenor.

On auctions: para 37 requires adequate notice to the borrower or legal heir, and where the borrower cannot be located, one month must lapse from the date of the public notice before the sale; para 38 requires the auction to be announced by advertisements in at least two newspapers, one in the regional language and one in a national daily; para 39 puts the auction through the lender's trained employee or an empanelled auctioneer; para 40 sets a reserve price not less than 90% of the current value, with 85% only after two failed auctions; para 41 requires the first auction to be held physically in the same district as the lending branch, with an adjoining district or an online auction only if the first fails; para 42 bars the lender and its related parties from bidding; and para 43 requires full details of the value fetched and the dues adjusted to go to the borrower, with any surplus refunded within seven working days of receiving the full auction proceeds.

Today's lead story sits directly under these paragraphs. The 1,853-account notices, as counted by InsolvencyTracker, ran as newspaper advertisements — para 38's requirement — and the notices follow the advertising and district rules; they publish no reserve price, which para 40 does not require them to publish. Whether the underlying loans were sanctioned before or after 1 April 2026 — which decides whether the Directions or the earlier rules apply — is not stated in the sources checked. The competing read in today's second story also turns on this rulebook: para 15's 12-month cap on bullet-repayment consumption loans is what a three-year EMI gold book sits outside by construction, and para 20's ongoing LTV requirement is where the 75% band presses on a bullet book as the collateral reprices.

Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: these Directions are the instrument Muthoot Fincorp's own auction practice runs under; they set the conduct for every gold-loan auction it holds, including the December rounds counted in today's lead story, and they give the borrower the notice, the district-level sale and the seven-working-day surplus refund that make the step transparent.
