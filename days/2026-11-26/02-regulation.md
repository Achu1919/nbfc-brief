### SEBI: half-yearly review of 'Significant Indices' — the Annexure-A list is unchanged

SEBI issued circular **HO/47/17/12(53)2026-MRD-POD5/I/26407/2026 on 25 November 2026**, the half-yearly review of 'Significant Indices' under the SEBI (Index Providers) Regulations, 2024. An index comes under the review when the daily average cumulative AUM tracking it across mutual fund schemes exceeds **₹20,000 crore for each of the past six months**, measured to the half-year ends of 30 June and 31 December. For the review period **1 January to 30 June 2026, the Annexure-A list is unchanged**; two indices — **BSE 200 (Sr. No. 2) and Nifty CPSE (Sr. No. 33)** — did not meet the threshold but are **retained under Para 5 of the May 2026 circular**. The circular is **effective immediately**, and its scope is **index providers, stock exchanges, mutual funds, AMCs and AMFI**.

**It is not an NBFC instrument, and it changes nothing on the list it reviews.** Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: none — the company is not an index provider, an exchange, a mutual fund or an AMC, and nothing in the circular addresses NBFC lending.

### SEBI: simplification of client on-boarding at KYC Registration Agencies — binds Section 12 intermediaries, in force 24 December 2026
<!-- reg: ref=HO/38/14/(25)2026-MIRSD-POD3; regulator=SEBI; issued=2026-11-25; effective=2026-12-24; status=new; lifecycle=final; applies=intermediaries -->
<!-- story: id=reg-sebi-kra-onboarding-2026-11 -->
SEBI issued circular **HO/38/14/(25)2026-MIRSD-POD3 on 25 November 2026** — "Simplification of client on-boarding and rationalisation of risk management framework at KYC Registration Agencies" — amending the KYC Master Circular (SEBI/HO/MIRSD/SECFATF/P/CIR/2023/169, 12 October 2023). It **centralises supplementary client information at KRA level**: the Part II account-opening-form data that is common across intermediaries, plus **CKYC ID, DIGIPIN — a 10-character geo-coded postal address — and OVD expiry date**. Intermediaries must upload Part I plus the Part II items — income range, FATCA/CRS, PEP status, occupation, CKYC ID, DIGIPIN and OVD expiry — **within three working days of completing KYC**. KRAs are to **tag validated and unvalidated records attribute-wise**, and intermediaries must seek additional information where a fetched record is unvalidated. The changes also apply to **individual Person Resident Outside India (PROI) clients from FATF-compliant countries** (paras 3.1–3.4, 3.6 and 3.7). **The circular comes into force on 24 December 2026** — thirty days from issue — and KRAs are to issue an SOP within the same 30 days.

Its scope is explicit: **intermediaries registered under Section 12 of the SEBI Act, recognised stock exchanges, clearing corporations and depositories. It is not addressed to NBFCs as lenders.** A gold-loan NBFC is not a Section 12 intermediary unless separately registered — for example as a depository participant — so this circular does not bind Muthoot Fincorp's lending business; its relevance runs to group or affiliate entities that are SEBI-registered intermediaries, and to KYC data quality generally. Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: none on the lending business; the company's KYC obligations as an NBFC arise from the RBI's framework, not from this circular.
Sources: [SEBI circular, 25 Nov 2026](https://www.sebi.gov.in/legal/circulars/nov-2026/simplification-of-client-on-boarding-and-rationalisation-of-risk-management-framework-at-kyc-registration-agencies_105839.html)

## 📖 The standing rulebook: the LTV bands, unchanged; AGLOC's 26 November block is a fresh move
<!-- story: id=reg-rbi-rbi-2025-26-47 -->
The gold-loan LTV bands that today's margin-call reporting restates are the standing ones: **85% for loans up to ₹2.5 lakh, 80% for loans between ₹2.5 and 5 lakh, 75% above ₹5 lakh**, as set out in Mint's 25 November report of the notices going out under them. **No RBI instrument dated 24, 25 or 26 November changes them, and no instrument of any regulator dated those days changes any other rule applying to Muthoot Fincorp's lending business.** AGLOC's block dated **26 November 2026** publishes the operative bands per gram — **₹9,424 at 85%, ₹8,869 at 80%, ₹8,315 at 75%, on a jewellery reference of ₹11,087** — and, unlike the 25 November block, it rests on a fresh reference: ₹11,087 is IBJA's 25 November PM 916 rate ÷ 10, not the 23 November print carried over the holiday. The changes from the previous block are **−₹304 at 85%, −₹287 at 80% and −₹268 at 75%, with the reference down ₹358**; the three bands move together because they are fixed percentages of the one reference — one observation, not three — and the percentages are arithmetic on AGLOC's own two blocks, not figures the page states.

Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: these bands and this reference govern the ceiling the company lends under; nothing in them changed today, and the fresh reference is a market print, not a rule move.
