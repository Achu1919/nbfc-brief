### Bureau data put gold loans at ₹22.5 lakh crore, with 90-day overdues up to 0.3%
<!-- story: id=gold-loan-asset-quality-2026 -->
<!-- tags: topics=gold-loan,asset-quality; category=asset-quality -->
The Economic Times reports that outstanding gold loans hit a record ₹22.5 lakh crore in August 2026, almost three times the August 2023 level, while loans overdue by more than 90 days rose to 0.3% of the portfolio from 0.2% in March. TransUnion CIBIL data show ₹8.2 lakh crore of gold loans sanctioned in April–June, up 12% year on year, even as the number of loans originated fell 15% to 3.7 crore.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: the system data match what the lenders said on their calls: tickets are getting bigger and early delinquency is creeping up from a low base. ET also cites Macquarie's warning that some lenders are giving fresh gold loans to borrowers already overdue elsewhere, which puts bureau checks on gold borrowers on every analyst's list, including for Muthoot Fincorp.
Sources: [Economic Times: gold-loan boom and overdues](https://m.economictimes.com/industry/banking/finance/whats-under-all-that-glitter-glare-falls-on-booming-gold-loan-business-gold-loan-interest-rate-gold-prices/amp_articleshow/135800635.cms) · [Economic Times: lenders and short-tenure loans](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/satet-front/dodging-bullet-loans-hurt-gold-nbfcs/articleshow/135817183.cms)

### Gold lenders end a four-session week mixed as Manappuram drops 5.35%
<!-- story: id=markets-daily-close -->
<!-- tags: companies=muthoot-finance,manappuram,iifl-finance,shriram-finance,bajaj-finance; topics=markets; category=markets -->
Between the 6 Nov and 13 Nov NSE closes (10 Nov was a trading holiday), the Nifty 50 rose 0.46% to 25,144.30 and Nifty Financial Services gained 2.09% to 27,855.50. Manappuram fell 5.35% to ₹308.00 and Muthoot Finance 1.01% to ₹2,840.00, while Bajaj Finance rose 2.69% to ₹1,117.00, IIFL Finance 2.45% to ₹641.10 and Shriram Finance 0.92% to ₹970.00.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: the two pure gold lenders lagged the financials index in the week Manappuram reported its Stage 2 jump and gold fell about 4.5%, while the diversified lenders rose. ## 🔄 Deals & M&A

### Godrej Capital raises ₹2,500 crore from Temasek as NBFCs line up fresh equity
<!-- story: id=nbfc-foreign-investment-2026 -->
<!-- tags: companies=godrej-capital,bajaj-finance,iifl-finance; topics=funding,m-and-a; category=deals -->
Godrej Industries told the exchanges on 12 Nov that Temasek affiliate Jongsong Investments will put ₹2,500 crore into Godrej Capital at a valuation of up to ₹28,000 crore, leaving the Godrej group with about 80%, subject to regulatory approvals. Financial Express lists it alongside Bajaj Finance's ₹5,800 crore of promoter warrants, IIFL Finance's ₹6,414 crore raise and Piramal Finance's ₹1,750 crore of warrants, while ICRA's Deep Inder Singh said the agency does not see a broad-based requirement for fresh equity capital across the industry at this stage.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: Godrej Capital lists gold loans among its products and had ₹35,000 crore of AUM at 31 October, per Business Standard, so another group-backed lender in gold now has fresh capital. Muthoot Fincorp's planned ₹3,000 crore fresh issue would come to market in the same queue of NBFC equity raises.
Sources: [Godrej Industries Reg 30 filing](https://www.godrejindustries.com/uploads/BSENSECOMPANYANNOUNCEMENT_12112026_FSIGNED_20a7a6b166.pdf) · [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/companies/news/godrej-capital-raises-2-500-crore-from-temasek-at-28-000-crore-valuation-126111200763_1.html) · [Financial Express](https://www.financialexpress.com/business/banking-finance-nbfcs-turn-to-warrants-to-fund-next-phase-of-growth-4382436/) · [BusinessLine: Muthoot Fincorp funding and IPO](https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/news/muthoot-fincorp-diversifies-funding-to-trim-borrowing-cost/article71695055.ece)
