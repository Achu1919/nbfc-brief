### [NEW] RBI cancels the registrations of 49 NBFCs
<!-- reg: ref=Press Release 2026-2027/1501; regulator=RBI; applies=nbfcs; issued=2026-11-16; status=in-force; lifecycle=final; title=RBI cancels Certificate of Registration of 49 NBFCs -->
RBI cancelled the certificates of registration of 49 NBFCs under Section 45-IA(6) of the RBI Act, through orders dated 1 to 29 October 2026; 43 of them have registered offices in West Bengal, 41 in Kolkata. RBI did not give the grounds for each cancellation, and the companies can no longer carry on non-banking financial business.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: none directly. Any business partner, co-lending counterparty or loan-sourcing entity that relies on an NBFC registration should be checked against RBI's list.
Reference: Press Release 2026-2027/1501 · Addressed to: public (NBFCs named) · Issued: 16 Nov 2026

### [NEW] 13 NBFCs and one HFC surrender their registrations, five under the Type I exemption
<!-- reg: ref=Press Release 2026-2027/1500; regulator=RBI; applies=nbfcs,hfcs; issued=2026-11-16; status=in-force; lifecycle=final; title=13 NBFCs and 1 HFC surrender Certificates of Registration -->
RBI cancelled the registrations of 13 NBFCs and one housing finance company that surrendered them: six left the business, five qualified as unregistered Type I NBFCs, and three ceased to exist after a merger, dissolution or strike-off. The Type I route is open to NBFCs with assets under ₹1,000 crore that take no public funds and have no customer interface.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: none. Muthoot Fincorp takes public funds and deals with customers, so it stays a registered NBFC; the exemption is relevant only to small group investment companies.
Reference: Press Release 2026-2027/1500 · Addressed to: public (NBFCs named) · Issued: 16 Nov 2026
Sources: [RBI press release](https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx?prid=63978) · [ETBFSI](https://bfsi.economictimes.indiatimes.com/articles/rbi-cancels-registrations-of-43-west-bengal-nbfcs-41-in-kolkata-nine-share-one-address/135900686)

### [NEW] SEBI forms a PMAC sub-group to review the ICDR Regulations
<!-- story: id=sebi-icdr-review-2026; dup -->
<!-- reg: ref=PR No.73/2026; regulator=SEBI; applies=listed-entities,issuers,merchant-bankers; issued=2026-11-16; status=new; title=SEBI sub-group of PMAC to review ICDR Regulations -->
This is a review, not a rule change. SEBI's Primary Market Advisory Committee sub-group, chaired by Ashish Chauhan of NSE, will review Chapters II, IIA, IV, V and VI of the ICDR Regulations (IPOs, FPOs, preferential issues, QIPs), covering eligibility, lock-in, allotment, disclosures, pricing and due-diligence certificates.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: Muthoot Fincorp's IPO offer documents are governed by Chapter II; any change would come first as a SEBI consultation paper, with no date given.
Reference: PR No.73/2026 · Addressed to: public · Issued: 16 Nov 2026
Sources: [SEBI PR No. 73/2026](https://www.sebi.gov.in/media-and-notifications/press-releases/nov-2026/sebi-constitutes-a-sub-group-of-pmac-to-review-specified-provisions-of-the-sebi-icdr-regulations-2018_105790.html) · [SEBI PR PDF](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/attachdocs/nov-2026/1794829566875.pdf)
