Bajaj Finance's QIP committee closed the issue late on Monday 16 Nov and allocated 114,146,341 equity shares to qualified institutional buyers at ₹1,025 each, above the ₹992.25 floor price set when the issue opened on 11 Nov. At that price the allocation works out to about ₹11,700 crore, the amount the board had approved for the QIP.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: the largest NBFC has just priced fresh equity above its floor in a week when foreign investors were net buyers, which is the reference point institutions will carry into other NBFC equity offers, including Muthoot Fincorp's planned ₹3,000 crore IPO.

### SEBI sets up a group to rewrite the IPO, QIP and preferential-issue rules
<!-- story: id=sebi-icdr-review-2026; single_source=official -->
<!-- tags: regulators=sebi; topics=policy,ipo; category=regulation -->
SEBI said on 16 Nov it has formed a sub-group of its Primary Market Advisory Committee, chaired by NSE MD and CEO Ashish Chauhan, to review Chapters II, IIA, IV, V and VI of the ICDR Regulations covering IPOs, FPOs, preferential issues and QIPs. Its terms of reference include eligibility conditions, promoter and pre-issue lock-in, allocation and allotment, offer-document disclosures, pricing and price discovery, and lead managers' due-diligence certificates.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: Muthoot Fincorp's IPO is at the draft red herring prospectus stage, so changes to disclosure, lock-in or price-band rules could apply to its offer documents if they take effect before the issue. SEBI has given no timeline, and any change would first come as a consultation paper.
Sources: [SEBI PR No. 73/2026](https://www.sebi.gov.in/media-and-notifications/press-releases/nov-2026/sebi-constitutes-a-sub-group-of-pmac-to-review-specified-provisions-of-the-sebi-icdr-regulations-2018_105790.html)

### Update: Gold hits a one-year low; AGLOC's 85% ceiling falls to ₹10,508 a gram
<!-- story: id=gold-price-weekly -->
<!-- tags: topics=gold-price,gold-loan; category=markets -->
Spot gold touched $3,830 an ounce on Monday, its lowest since October 2025, and was at $3,852.95 at 3:38 p.m. New York time, as markets priced a December Fed hike and the dollar held near a 19-month high; gold is down 31.5% from January's record. AGLOC's new period for 17 Nov sets the 85% ceiling at ₹10,508 a gram on a jewellery reference of ₹12,363, down from ₹10,574 and ₹12,440 for 14–16 Nov.

### RBI tells Tata Sons again that Upper Layer rules, including listing, apply
<!-- story: id=rbi-tata-sons-upper-layer-2026 -->

### Update: CRIF High Mark sees a small rise in gold-loan overdues since March, but no stress
<!-- story: id=gold-loan-asset-quality-2026 -->
<!-- tags: topics=gold-loan,asset-quality; category=asset-quality -->
CRIF High Mark's South Asia MD Sachin Seth told the Economic Times that gold-loan repayment is slightly better than a year ago, with "a small uptick in overdues since March among some lenders and in larger loans". He put industry gold-loan growth at about 60% and said the bureau sees no systemic issue.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: this matches the March-to-August rise in delinquencies that TransUnion CIBIL reported (covered 15 Nov). Bureaus say the stress is concentrated in larger tickets, which is where Muthoot Fincorp's mix will get compared with its peers'.
Sources: [Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/markets/expert-view/lenders-strike-gold-amid-a-broader-shift-to-secured-credit/articleshow/135869576.cms)

## 🏭 Sector Deep Dive — Tuesday: Gold loan sector

**Price.** Gold has fallen hard: spot touched $3,830 an ounce on Monday, down 31.5% from January's record of $5,594.82 ([Economic Times (Reuters)](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/markets/us-stocks/wall-street-guide/gold-price-slides-to-one-year-low-heads-for-worst-year-since-2015/articleshow/135902147.cms)). In India, IBJA's 999 rate was ₹1,34,971 per 10 g on Monday afternoon, against ₹1,37,692 on 12 Nov ([IBJA](https://ibjarates.com/)). AGLOC's per-gram ceilings have come down in step: the 85% figure was ₹10,720 for 13 Nov, ₹10,574 for 14–16 Nov and is ₹10,508 today ([AGLOC](https://www.agloc.org/)).

**Credit.** Bureaus see the book still growing fast, about 60% across the industry by CRIF High Mark's estimate, with overdues edging up since March, mostly among some lenders and in larger loans ([Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/markets/expert-view/lenders-strike-gold-amid-a-broader-shift-to-secured-credit/articleshow/135869576.cms)). Muthoot Fincorp's own Stage III fell to 1.07% on 30 September from 1.41% a year earlier ([BusinessLine](https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/money-and-banking/ipo-bound-muthoot-fincorp-q2-profit-doubles-to-779-cr-asset-quality-improves/article71707222.ece)).

**Capital and funding.** Large lenders are raising equity as gold prices fall: Bajaj Finance closed its QIP at ₹1,025 a share on Monday ([NSE](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/BAJFINANCE_16112026234915_BSENSE_Intimation_Issue_closing_pricing.pdf)), and IIFL Finance's preferential-issue EGM is on 4 Dec. A falling collateral price combined with fresh competitor capital is the setting for Muthoot Fincorp's H2 growth plan and its IPO.
