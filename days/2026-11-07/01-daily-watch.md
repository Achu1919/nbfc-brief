# 📊 NBFC Daily Watch — Saturday, November 7, 2026

Morning edition for Saturday, 7 November 2026 (IST). Covers 6 Nov session and overnight. Indian equity markets closed today.

## 🔴 URGENT / High Impact

### RBI: NBFC credit up 15.4%; gold-jewellery loans up 66.1% to ₹3.74 lakh crore
<!-- tags: regulators=rbi; topics=gold-loan,funding,macro; category=urgent -->
RBI's 6 Nov release on sectoral deployment of NBFC credit for September 2026 shows overall NBFC (incl. HFC) credit up 15.4% YoY to ₹61.70 lakh crore, vs 13.3% a year earlier. Within retail, loans against gold jewellery rose 66.1% YoY to ₹3.74 lakh crore; retail loans overall grew 21.9% to ₹26.84 lakh crore.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: official confirmation that gold jewellery is the fastest retail pocket in the Upper and Middle Layer NBFC sample, which sets the system benchmark for gold-loan AUM growth comparisons.
Source: [RBI](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx?prid=63940)

## 🏘️ Sector Deep Dive — Saturday: Infrastructure & project finance

### RBI: infrastructure lending at NBFCs slowed to 5.9% YoY in September
<!-- tags: topics=funding,macro; category=sector -->
Inside industry credit (up 7.4% YoY to ₹22.69 lakh crore), RBI says infrastructure growth stayed subdued at 5.9% YoY to ₹20.60 lakh crore, vs 9.4% a year earlier; power lending grew 7.9% to ₹14.80 lakh crore. ETBFSI's read of the same release puts infrastructure at those levels and contrasts it with gold jewellery's 66.1% surge.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: none direct on the gold book — system growth is rotating toward retail secured (gold, housing, vehicles) while classic infra NBFCs are the slow pocket.
Source: [RBI](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx?prid=63940)
