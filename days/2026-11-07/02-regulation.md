## 🔔 New Regulatory Updates Today

### [NEW] RBI publishes September 2026 NBFC sectoral credit; gold jewellery loans +66.1%
<!-- reg: ref=Press Release (prid=63940); regulator=RBI; applies=nbfcs,hfcs; issued=2026-11-06; status=new; title=Sectoral Deployment of Credit by NBFC – September 2026 -->
RBI on 6 Nov released sectoral deployment of credit by NBFCs (Upper and Middle Layer sample plus HFCs, ~87% of sector credit) for September 2026. Overall credit grew 15.4% YoY to ₹61.70 lakh crore; retail grew 21.9% to ₹26.84 lakh crore. ETBFSI's read of the release puts loans against gold jewellery at ₹3.74 lakh crore, up 66.1% YoY; infrastructure lending grew a subdued 5.9%.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: the system gold print is the benchmark for gold-loan AUM growth comparisons across Upper and Middle Layer NBFCs.
Reference: RBI press release (prid=63940) · Addressed to: public (data release on UL/ML NBFCs and HFCs) · Issued: 6 Nov 2026
Source: [RBI](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx?prid=63940)

### [NEW] SEBI (Settlement of Administrative and Civil Proceedings) Regulations, 2026 commence 7 Nov
<!-- reg: ref=PR No.70/2026; regulator=SEBI; applies=listed-entities,intermediaries; issued=2026-11-06; effective=2026-11-07; status=new; title=Commencement of SEBI Settlement Regulations, 2026 -->
SEBI PR No.70/2026 (6 Nov) confirms the Settlement Regulations, 2026 — notified 8 Oct 2026 — come into force on 7 Nov 2026. FAQs, an online settlement-amount calculator, an online application form and a help desk are available on sebi.gov.in. ET reports a 90-day filing window from show-cause (or 90 days from 7 Nov for pending matters), application fees of ₹25,000 (individuals) / ₹35,000 (others), and a settlement-notice route before SCN in some cases.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: not a lending or gold-loan circular. It matters for any future SEBI civil or administrative exposure on listed NCDs or related intermediary conduct.
Reference: PR No.70/2026 · Addressed to: market participants / eligible settlement applicants · Issued: 6 Nov 2026 · Effective: 7 Nov 2026
Source: [SEBI](https://www.sebi.gov.in/media-and-notifications/press-releases/nov-2026/commencement-of-sebi-settlement-of-administrative-and-civil-proceedings-regulations-2026_105680.html)

### No new NBFC-lending circular from RBI notifications, IBBI, NHB or FIU-IND on 6 Nov
RBI's notifications RSS after the 5 Nov Master Directions on Cyber Resilience for non-bank payment system operators has no fresh NBFC-lending instrument dated 6 Nov. IBBI what's-new, NHB notifications and FIU-IND homepage checked this morning; nothing new for gold-loan NBFCs overnight. Bank-facing items on 6 Nov were routine (VRR/VRRR, T-bill and G-sec auctions, FLA census, Bulletin extract, one co-op bank direction).
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: none today beyond the data release and SEBI settlement commencement above.
Source: [RBI notifications](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx)
