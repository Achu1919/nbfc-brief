### Gold jumps over 3% after the US midterms; listed gold lenders rally up to 7%
<!-- tags: companies=muthoot-finance,manappuram,iifl-finance; topics=gold-loan,gold-price,markets; category=urgent -->
Spot gold was up 3.4% at $4,059.95 an ounce at 19:04 GMT on 4 Nov, heading for its biggest daily gain since mid-August, as the dollar fell after Democrats won both houses of the US Congress. On NSE, Muthoot Finance (peer) closed 7.18% higher at ₹2,709.80, IIFL Finance rose 6.77% and Manappuram Finance 3.13%.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: AGLOC's 85% LTV ceiling for today rose ₹315 to ₹11,026 a gram, which raises the maximum per-gram amount gold lenders may lend today. The rally coincides with the last day of Muthoot Fincorp's NCD issue.
Source: [Reuters via Investing.com](https://au.investing.com/news/economy-news/gold-firms-on-softer-oil-prices-us-midterm-results-awaited-4739840)

## 🏢 Muthoot Fincorp in the market

### Muthoot Capital Services among October's larger NBFC bond issuers
<!-- tags: companies=muthoot-capital; topics=funding,ncd; category=muthoot-fincorp -->
ETBFSI Research lists Muthoot Capital Services, a separate listed Muthoot Pappachan Group company, alongside Bajaj Finance, Poonawalla Fincorp, Sundaram Finance and Jio Credit as the largest NBFC private-placement borrowers in October. NBFCs as a whole raised ₹24,457 crore, down 19% from September.

### SEBI moves KYC for non-resident individual clients online
<!-- tags: regulators=sebi; topics=regulation,kyc; category=regulation -->
SEBI circular HO/38/30/12(3)2026-MIRSD-POD2/I/25175/2026 of 4 Nov lets market intermediaries complete KYC digitally for individual clients resident outside India, without the client being physically in India. It applies from 3 Dec 2026.

<!-- tags: topics=funding,bonds,rates; category=deals -->
NBFCs raised ₹24,457 crore through private placements in October, down from ₹30,034 crore in September, and AAA issuers took about 68% of all corporate bond money. ## 🏘️ Sector Deep Dive — Thursday: MSME & commercial lending

### Update: NBFCs swap revolving MSME lines for term loans before RBI's final rule
<!-- tags: companies=bajaj-finance,tata-capital,cholamandalam,shriram-finance,aditya-birla-capital; regulators=rbi; topics=msme,revolving-credit,regulation; category=msme -->
ET reports that Bajaj Finance started term-loan variants without redraw on 1 Nov, and Tata Capital is piloting replacements for its flexi loans, about 90% of which go to MSMEs. Shriram Finance's Umesh Revankar puts revolving credit across NBFCs at about ₹2 lakh crore against a total book of roughly ₹30 lakh crore.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: business-loan products with a redraw feature, at any lender, would need term-loan versions if RBI's August draft becomes final.
Source: [The Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/industry/banking/finance/nbfcs-redesign-lending-models-ahead-of-revolving-credit-curbs/articleshow/135512499.cms)

### Lendingkart lifts its MSME ticket size to ₹17 lakh and plans LAP
<!-- tags: companies=lendingkart; topics=msme,lap,unsecured-lending; category=msme -->
CRISIL's 4 Nov rationale says Lendingkart's average ticket on new unsecured MSME loans rose to ₹17 lakh from ₹6–7 lakh, with physical checks added to credit appraisal. It plans a secured loan-against-property product this fiscal.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: fintech MSME lenders are moving up-ticket and into LAP, where Muthoot Fincorp's DRHP also plans growth, which points to more competition for the same small-business customers.
Source: [CRISIL Ratings](https://www.crisilratings.com/mnt/winshare/Ratings/RatingList/RatingDocs/LendingkartFinanceLimited_November%2004_%202026_RR_409071.html)

### Five-Star: record ₹2,078 crore disbursal, 1% higher yields on new loans
<!-- tags: companies=five-star; topics=msme,results,lap; category=msme -->
Five-Star Business Finance's Q2 call transcript, filed on 30 Oct, reports record disbursements of ₹2,078 crore (up 74% year on year), AUM of ₹14,640 crore and PAT of ₹299 crore. Gross Stage 3 was 3.48% and 30+ DPD fell to 11.5% from 12.38%. Yields on new loans went up by 1% from October to offset rising funding costs.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: a large small-business LAP lender is repricing upward by about 100 bps. That sets a higher pricing reference for small-ticket LAP across the segment, including Muthoot Fincorp's.
Source: [NSE filing (Five-Star)](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/FIVESTAR_30102026113317_TranscriptofEarningsConferenceCall.pdf)

### MSME lender stocks: UGRO +3.79%, SBFC +0.64%, Five-Star flat
<!-- tags: companies=ugro-capital,sbfc,five-star; topics=msme,markets; category=msme -->
On 4 Nov UGRO Capital closed at ₹70.92 (+3.79%), SBFC Finance at ₹88.25 (+0.64%) and Five-Star at ₹397.15 (+0.01%). All three trailed the 1.40% rise in the Nifty Financial Services Ex-Bank index.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: the market is treating MSME lenders as rate-sensitive this week, behind the gold lenders.
Source: [NSE bhavcopy](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/products/content/sec_bhavdata_full_04112026.csv)

> **Read for Muthoot Fincorp:** Two things are changing in MSME lending at once. RBI's revolving-credit draft is pushing NBFCs to replace redraw facilities with term loans, and rising yields are leading lenders such as Five-Star to raise rates on new loans by about 1%. The MSME, LAP and business-loan lines in Muthoot Fincorp's DRHP face both: product terms that may need rewriting and higher funding costs. Peers' rate increases indicate that higher pricing is being passed through in the segment.
