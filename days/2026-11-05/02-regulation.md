## 🔔 New Regulatory Updates Today

### SEBI allows digital KYC for individual clients resident outside India
<!-- reg: ref=HO/38/30/12(3)2026-MIRSD-POD2/I/25175/2026; regulator=SEBI; applies=intermediaries,kras,depositories; issued=2026-11-04; effective=2026-12-03; status=new; title=Review of Know Your Client process for individual Person Resident Outside India clients -->
SEBI's 4 Nov circular removes the effective requirement that a non-resident individual be in India to finish KYC, so intermediaries can onboard such clients digitally. Intermediaries must pass the KYC information to a KRA within 3 working days. The same process applies to re-KYC, and the circular takes effect for onboarding from 3 Dec 2026.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: no lending impact. Non-resident investors seeking its listed NCDs through a demat account may find onboarding easier from December.
Reference: HO/38/30/12(3)2026-MIRSD-POD2/I/25175/2026 · Addressed to: SEBI-registered intermediaries, stock exchanges, clearing corporations and depositories · Issued: 4 Nov 2026 · Effective: 3 Dec 2026
Source: [SEBI](https://www.sebi.gov.in/legal/circulars/nov-2026/review-of-know-your-client-process-for-individual-person-resident-outside-india-clients_105603.html)

### No new NBFC instrument from RBI, IBBI, NHB or FIU-IND on 4 Nov
RBI's notification feed has nothing after FEMA 23(R)/(2)/2026-RB of 2 Nov, and its Master Directions page shows no update after 1 Oct. Its 4 Nov press releases were routine market operations plus the reconstitution of the Technical Consultative Committee for Financial Markets. IBBI's latest circulars are still the two of 30 Oct, and NHB's 4 Nov notice concerns its own term-deposit scheme.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: no new compliance action today.
Source: [RBI](https://www.rbi.org.in/scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx?prid=63914)
