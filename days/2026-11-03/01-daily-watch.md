# 📊 NBFC Daily Watch — Tuesday, November 3, 2026

*Tuesday edition, compiled before the market opens. Equity, index, rupee and flow numbers are Monday 2 November closes; gold spot and Brent are the latest prints. IBJA's 3 November AM rate had not been published at 07:13 IST, so the IBJA rows show Monday's PM rate. Muthoot Fincorp Ltd is a separate company from Muthoot Finance Ltd; Muthoot Finance appears here as a listed gold-loan lender.*

## 🔴 URGENT / High Impact

**1. Muthoot Finance hit a 52-week low, then closed up 4.4% as brokerages split on its Q2**
On Monday Muthoot Finance touched ₹2,305 on the NSE, a fresh 52-week low, then closed at ₹2,514.70, up ₹106.10 (+4.41%). ([NSE bhavcopy](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/products/content/sec_bhavdata_full_02112026.csv), [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/markets/news/muthoot-finance-rebounds-up-9-from-52-week-low-as-stock-reacts-to-q2-nos-126110200210_1.html)) Equirus upgraded it to Add from Reduce with a ₹2,920 target, citing valuation after the six-month fall, and raised its FY27 credit-cost assumption to 70 bp from 20 bp. Motilal Oswal stayed at Neutral with a ₹2,550 target. ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/markets/news/muthoot-finance-rebounds-up-9-from-52-week-low-as-stock-reacts-to-q2-nos-126110200210_1.html), [Moneycontrol](https://www.moneycontrol.com/news/business/neutral-muthoot-finance-target-of-rs-2550-motilal-oswal-14066378.html)) Mint sets the numbers side by side: gold AUM up 43% YoY and NIM 11.93% (+152 bp QoQ), but Stage 3 at ₹7,268 crore vs ₹3,926.5 crore in Q1 and Stage 2 at ₹23,466 crore vs ₹1,876 crore in Q1. FY27 gold-AUM growth guidance went to 25% from 15%, with NIM guidance of 10.5–11%. ([Mint](https://www.livemint.com/market/mark-to-market/muthoot-finance-share-price-gold-loan-asset-quality-concerns-11793602247502.html))
> **Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp:** Gold lenders are now being valued on growth and on the Stage 2 bucket together, so Muthoot Fincorp's Q2 Stage 2 figure (results due by 14 November) is likely to be read against this one.

**2. The gold collateral rate fell again: AGLOC's 85% LTV ceiling is ₹10,709/g today, down ₹195**
AGLOC's maximum loan per gram at 85% LTV is ₹10,709 for 3 November, against ₹10,904 for 31 October–2 November. Its jewellery reference price fell to ₹12,599 from ₹12,828. ([AGLOC](https://www.agloc.org/)) IBJA 999 closed Monday's PM session at ₹13,752.4/g, 1.81% below Friday's PM. ([IBJA](https://ibjarates.com/)) Overnight, spot gold rebounded 2.1% to $3,954.41/oz by 1849 GMT after Friday's one-year low, helped by the fall in oil. ([MarketScreener/Reuters](https://ca.marketscreener.com/news/gold-rebounds-from-one-year-low-as-oil-slides-ahead-of-us-midterms-ce785fd8de8fff24))
> **Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp:** The per-gram ceiling caps fresh disbursement and top-ups for gold lenders including Muthoot Fincorp, so a lower rate means smaller ticket sizes on the same jewellery today.

**3. Oil fell 7.6% on Iran-deal hopes, and the rupee closed flat**
Brent settled at $104.77 on Monday, down 7.6%, and was trading at $104.50 on Tuesday morning. ([NDTV Profit](https://www.ndtvprofit.com/markets/oil-prices-today-november-3-brent-crude-oil-near-104-wti-8-percent-crash-us-iran-peace-hopes-ease-supply-fears-trump-commodity-prices-12274002)) The rupee closed at 94.90 per dollar against 94.95; traders see gains capped around 94.50 because of RBI's large forward book. ([BusinessLine/PTI](https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/markets/forex/rupee-ends-little-changed-as-traders-stay-cautious-despite-oil-dollar-drop/article71656235.ece))
> Lower oil eases the inflation pressure behind RBI's October rate hike, a factor in NBFC funding costs.

## 🏭 Sector Deep Dive — Tuesday: Gold loans

**1. Banks' gold book is now bigger than NBFCs'.** Bank gold loans grew 83% year on year to ₹5.77 lakh crore, with nearly ₹3 lakh crore added in 12 months. ([Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/latest/corporate/story/bt-explainer-gold-loans-surge-83-to-rs5-77-lakh-crore-what-happens-if-gold-prices-fall-15-564951-2026-11-01)) NBFC gold loans grew 68.5% to about ₹3.54 lakh crore in July, per RBI data cited by Supra Pacific's CEO. ([BusinessLine](https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/markets/gold/indias-gold-loan-market-is-entering-a-new-phase-of-growth/article71646198.ece))

**2. What a 15% fall does.** In Business Today's example, a loan at 65% LTV moves to about 76.5% if gold falls 15% and the balance stays the same. Equirus's Shreepal Doshi said a sharp fall could push up NPAs and credit costs, but does not expect a 20–25% drop from here. ([Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/latest/corporate/story/bt-explainer-gold-loans-surge-83-to-rs5-77-lakh-crore-what-happens-if-gold-prices-fall-15-564951-2026-11-01))

**3. The rules are where the stress shows up.** Since 1 April, LTV is capped at 85% for loans under ₹2.5 lakh, 80% up to ₹5 lakh and 75% above that. Muthoot Finance's MD ties the rise in its overdues to the short-tenure, monthly-EMI products that followed. ([Financial Express](https://www.financialexpress.com/business/news/short-tenure-loans-monthly-emis-drive-up-gold-loan-npas-muthoot-finance-md/4374640/lite/))

**4. The collateral rate.** AGLOC's three tiers for 3 November are ₹10,709, ₹10,079 and ₹9,449 per gram, all down from the 31 October–2 November period. Its jewellery reference price (₹12,599) matches IBJA's 22K PM rate on 2 November (₹12,599.0/g). ([AGLOC](https://www.agloc.org/), [IBJA](https://ibjarates.com/))

**5. Competition and growth guidance.** Muthoot Finance raised its FY27 gold-AUM growth guidance to 25%; Motilal Oswal flags a growth-versus-yields trade-off as AAA and AA+ lenders enter the segment. ([Mint](https://www.livemint.com/market/mark-to-market/muthoot-finance-share-price-gold-loan-asset-quality-concerns-11793602247502.html))

> **Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp:** As a gold-loan NBFC under the same April 2026 rules, Muthoot Fincorp's Q2 disclosures on LTV by ticket band and Stage 2 by product are the figures most comparable with this week's sector numbers.
