## 🔔 New Regulatory Updates Today

### [DRAFT] RBI consolidates Priority Sector Lending instructions into five bank-wise draft Directions
<!-- reg: ref=Press Release 2026-2027/1447; regulator=RBI; applies=banks; issued=2026-11-05; effective=; status=draft; title=Consolidation of Instructions Related to Priority Sector Lending – Drafts for Comments -->
RBI on 5 Nov placed five draft Priority Sector Lending Directions on its website — one each for commercial banks, small finance banks, local area banks, regional rural banks and urban co-operative banks — and invited comments by 26 Nov 2026. The drafts consolidate existing PSL targets and classification rules; ETBFSI reports they retain the overall PSL targets and the 5% cap on eligible bank lending through NBFCs for on-lending.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: no new NBFC Direction today, but the retained 5% on-lending cap is the ceiling that shapes how much PSL-eligible bank money can reach gold and other NBFC books through the on-lending channel.
Reference: Press Release 2026-2027/1447 · Addressed to: commercial banks, SFBs, LABs, RRBs, UCBs (draft Directions) · Issued: 5 Nov 2026 · Comments by: 26 Nov 2026
Source: [RBI](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx?prid=63925)

### SEBI circulars on 5 Nov: accredited investors, MF net settlement, currency-derivatives limits
<!-- tags: regulators=sebi; topics=circular; category=regulation -->
SEBI issued three circulars on 5 Nov: HO/19/34/11(6)2026-AFD-POD1/I/25244/2026 (accredited-investor framework), HO/24/13/(1)2026-IMD-POD-2/I/25242/2026 (net settlement of funds for mutual-fund cash-market trades), and HO/(561)2026-MRD-POD2/I/25177/2026 (currency-derivatives underlying-exposure limits). None is addressed to NBFCs as lenders.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: none for lending or NCD disclosure. Listed only so the circulars list stays complete.
Source: [SEBI — accredited investors](https://www.sebi.gov.in/legal/circulars/nov-2026/review-of-the-framework-for-accredited-investors_105618.html)

### No new NBFC instrument from RBI notifications, IBBI, NHB or FIU-IND on 5 Nov
RBI's notification feed has nothing after FEMA 23(R)/(2)/2026-RB of 2 Nov. The Master Directions page shows no fresh "Updated as on" change for NBFC Directions beyond prior checks. IBBI and NHB pages returned no new NBFC circular dated 5 Nov in this morning's fetch.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: no new compliance action from those channels today.
