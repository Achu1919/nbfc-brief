### Muthoot Fincorp Tranche I NCD closes; bids stood at 2.67x
<!-- tags: companies=muthoot-fincorp; topics=ncd,funding; category=muthoot-fincorp -->
The Tranche I public NCD closed on 5 Nov with the book at 2.67x the ₹200 crore base at 17:00 IST, up from 2.55x a day earlier. Retail was at 2.51x, HNI 3.79x, non-institutional 1.34x and institutional 0.02x.

### Muthoot Microfin Q2 PAT ₹110.8 crore; gold co-lending with Muthoot Fincorp at ₹453 crore
<!-- tags: companies=muthoot-microfin,muthoot-fincorp; topics=results,microfinance,gold-loan; category=urgent -->
Muthoot Microfin, a Muthoot Fincorp subsidiary, reported Q2 FY27 PAT of ₹110.8 crore, up 262.9% YoY, on total income of ₹732.9 crore. Disbursements hit a record ₹2,900 crore and GLP rose 22% to ₹15,323 crore; the release says gold loans through referrals and co-lending with Muthoot Fincorp disbursed ₹453 crore in the quarter.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: the subsidiary's rebound feeds the consolidated Q2 pack due 14 Nov, and the ₹453 crore co-lending line is a direct volume and fee read for Muthoot Fincorp's gold book.
Source: [NSE press release (Muthoot Microfin)](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/muthootmicrofin_05112026171622_MMLQ2FY27PressReleaseSD.pdf)

### RBI puts bank PSL draft Directions out for comment; 5% NBFC on-lending cap retained
<!-- tags: regulators=rbi; topics=circular,funding,policy; category=urgent -->
RBI on 5 Nov published five draft Priority Sector Lending Directions for banks (commercial banks, SFBs, LABs, RRBs, UCBs), consolidating existing PSL rules. Comments are due by 26 Nov. ETBFSI reports the drafts retain the overall PSL targets and the 5% cap on eligible bank lending through NBFCs for on-lending.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: the on-lending cap stays the binding constraint on how much PSL-eligible bank funding can reach gold and other NBFC books through the on-lending channel; no new headroom in this draft.
Source: [RBI press release](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx?prid=63925)

### AMU Leasing's commercial-EV book reaches 14,000 customers across 10 states
<!-- tags: topics=vehicle-finance,digital-lending; category=sector -->
AMU Leasing, an RBI-registered NBFC focused on commercial EVs, said it has about 14,000 customers across 10 states, with non-metro markets at 65% of disbursements, and plans to enter Rajasthan and Chhattisgarh. The portfolio covers e-2Ws, e-3Ws, passenger and commercial EVs plus battery solutions.
