### Update: NBFCs weigh higher rates and fees to offset IRDAI's proposed insurance-commission caps
<!-- tags: regulators=irdai; topics=policy,housing-finance; companies=bajaj-housing-finance,home-first-finance; category=regulation -->
New since the 7 Nov edition, ET reports that Bajaj Housing Finance and Home First Finance are looking at higher processing fees, pricing and product-mix changes to make up lost insurance distribution income. Home First puts the pre-mitigation hit at about 35 bps of RoA, and an ICRA study cited by ET put insurance distribution income at 8% of NBFC PAT in FY26, up from 0.8% in FY23.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: lenders that earn insurance-referral fees alongside loans face the same pressure on fee income; housing lenders are the first to say they will pass it on through pricing.
Source: [Economic Times](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/markets/stocks/news/nbfcs-may-raise-loan-rates-fees-to-offset-insurance-income-cap/articleshow/135667186.cms)

## ⚠️ Credit Rating Actions

### CRISIL keeps Avendus Finance on Watch Positive pending the Mizuho deal; CP limit raised to ₹300 crore
<!-- tags: companies=avendus-finance; topics=rating,m-and-a; category=ratings -->
CRISIL on 6 Nov kept Avendus Finance's A+ long-term rating on Rating Watch with Positive Implications and reaffirmed A1+ on commercial paper, now enhanced to ₹300 crore from ₹200 crore. CRISIL says the rating could move up a notch or more once Mizuho Securities completes its stake purchase in parent Avendus Capital, which management expects by end-November subject to the NBFC licence approval still pending.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: no direct overlap with a wholesale lender; the case shows how a strategic parent can lift an Indian NBFC's rating.
Source: [CRISIL](https://www.crisilratings.com/mnt/winshare/Ratings/RatingList/RatingDocs/AvendusFinancePrivateLimited_November%2006_%202026_RR_409679.html)

## 🏭 Sector Deep Dive — Monday: Microfinance & SHG lending

### Banks pull back from microfinance as NBFC-MFIs take 44.7% of the market
<!-- tags: topics=microfinance,asset-quality; category=sector -->
CRIF High Mark data for September show the industry's gross loan portfolio down 5% YoY to ₹3.28 lakh crore, with banks' share falling to 21.8% from 31.3% as their book shrank 34%. NBFC-MFIs lifted their share to 44.7% from 38.8% and grew their book 9.4% to ₹1.47 lakh crore, while 31–90 dpd PAR held at 0.8% between June and September.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: its subsidiary Muthoot Microfin operates in the NBFC-MFI segment that is gaining share, and stable early delinquency is the backdrop for consolidated Q2 results. Average ticket size rose 17.5% to ₹63,100.
Source: [BusinessLine](https://www.thehindubusinessline.com/money-and-banking/banks-retreat-from-microfinance-ceding-ground-to-specialist-lenders-crif-high-mark/article71669670.ece)

### Update: Fusion Finance sees demand back in October but may trim its ₹10,000 crore AUM target
<!-- tags: companies=fusion-finance; topics=microfinance,results; category=sector -->
New since its Q2 results (covered 4 Nov), Fusion's MD Sanjay Garyali told Business Standard that floods across 140–150 branches in five states held back disbursements, so the FY27 AUM target of about ₹10,000 crore may moderate, though growth returned in October. He guided credit costs of 2–2.5%, said incremental borrowing costs have fallen to about 9.4% from 12.5–13%, and expects NIMs up about 20 bps in Q3.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: lower funding costs and recovering demand across NBFC-MFIs are relevant to Muthoot Microfin; weather-related disruption is a sector-wide factor for Q3.
Source: [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/finance/news/fusion-finance-targets-20-25-aum-growth-as-loan-demand-picks-up-in-h2fy27-126110800384_1.html)
