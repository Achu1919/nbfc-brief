# 📋 NBFC Weekly Analysis: Week of 2 – 8 November 2026

*Published Monday, 9 November 2026 (IST). Covers trading sessions Mon 2 to Fri 6 November, Sunday's Diwali Muhurat session (8 Nov) and weekend news. Muthoot Fincorp Ltd is a Middle Layer NBFC with unlisted equity and listed NCDs. Muthoot Finance Ltd is a separate, listed company; none of its numbers are Muthoot Fincorp's. Muthoot Capital Services and Muthoot Microfin are also separate listed companies (Muthoot Microfin is a Muthoot Fincorp subsidiary).*

- **Oil broke, and almost everything a lender cares about moved its way.** Front-month Brent fell 13.32% on the week to $98.32 after the Democratic sweep in the US midterms revived hopes of an Iran deal ([Dow Jones via Morningstar](https://www.morningstar.com/news/dow-jones/2026110610958/front-month-ice-brent-crude-fell-1332-this-week-to-settle-at-9832-data-talk)). The 10-year G-sec fell 22 bps to 7.1898%, its best week since October 2019, and swap markets cut the RBI hikes they price to 50–75 bps from about 125 bps ([ET/Reuters](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/markets/bonds/indias-10-year-bond-posts-best-week-in-7-years-leads-emerging-asia-as-oil-slides/articleshow/135606792.cms)).
- **Financials had their best week in years, and gold lenders led.** Nifty Financial Services rose 10.41% from the 30 Oct close to the 6 Nov close ([NSE index closes](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/content/indices/ind_close_all_06112026.csv)). FPIs bought a record ₹45,960 cr of Indian equities in the week while DIIs sold ₹24,197 cr ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/markets/news/street-basks-in-diwali-glow-benchmark-indices-post-best-week-in-6-years-126110602064_1.html)). - **RBI's own data now shows how big the gold-loan boom is.** NBFC loans against gold jewellery were up 66.1% year on year in September to ₹3.74 lakh cr, against 15.4% for all NBFC credit ([RBI](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx?prid=63940)). On the same days, ETBFSI added Bajaj Finance's gold Stage 2 build to the list of peers linking stress to the post-April repayment structures ([ETBFSI](https://bfsi.economictimes.indiatimes.com/articles/gold-loan-boom-raises-early-signs-of-borrower-stress-at-nbfcs/135478275)).
- **RBI's only real policy move was a draft, and it leaves NBFC on-lending where it was.** Five draft bank-wise Priority Sector Lending Directions went out on 5 Nov for comments by 26 Nov, and ETBFSI reports they keep the 5% cap on bank lending through NBFCs for on-lending ([RBI](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx?prid=63925), [ETBFSI](https://bfsi.economictimes.indiatimes.com/articles/rbi-reviews-priority-sector-lending-framework-retains-broad-targets-in-draft-norms/135578794)).
- **Muthoot Fincorp's week: Tranche I bids stood at 2.67× at close.** Bids on the ₹200 cr base came to about ₹534 cr against the ₹500 cr issue ceiling (retail 2.51×, HNI 3.79×, institutional 0.02×) ([Chittorgarh](https://www.chittorgarh.com/bond/muthoot-fincorp-tranche-i-ncd-october-2026/399/)). Subsidiary Muthoot Microfin's Q2 PAT rose 262.9% to ₹110.8 cr, and it reported ₹453 cr of gold loans disbursed through referrals and co-lending with Muthoot Fincorp in the quarter ([NSE filing](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/muthootmicrofin_05112026171622_MMLQ2FY27PressReleaseSD.pdf)). Allotment is due today, and the 10 Nov listing date falls on a market holiday ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/markets/news/samvat-2083-off-to-a-tepid-start-sensex-nifty-slip-smaller-cos-up-126110800423_1.html)).

## 🏛️ Regulatory & Policy Watch

Another quiet week for NBFC rules. RBI issued nothing addressed to NBFCs; its notifications feed ends with a 5 Nov Master Direction for payment system operators ([RBI notifications](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/NotificationUser.aspx)). The useful regulatory content was a bank-side draft and a data release.

**1. Draft PSL Directions for banks (5 Nov, comments by 26 Nov).** RBI Press Release 2026-2027/1447 puts out five draft Directions (commercial banks, SFBs, LABs, RRBs, UCBs) that consolidate existing PSL instructions ([RBI](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx?prid=63925)). ETBFSI reports the drafts keep the overall targets and the 5% cap on eligible bank lending to NBFCs for on-lending ([ETBFSI](https://bfsi.economictimes.indiatimes.com/articles/rbi-reviews-priority-sector-lending-framework-retains-broad-targets-in-draft-norms/135578794)).
- *Direct impact:* none on NBFC operations. The cap decides how much PSL-eligible bank money can reach NBFC books through the on-lending route.
- *Second order:* the consolidation is the chance for banks to lobby on co-lending and on-lending language. If the final text tightens classification, bank appetite for NBFC on-lending pools drops.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: no new headroom for any PSL on-lending or bank co-lending lines it uses; the draft is open for comment until 26 Nov.

**2. RBI's September NBFC credit data (6 Nov).** NBFC (incl. HFC) credit grew 15.4% to ₹61.70 lakh cr, retail loans 21.9% to ₹26.84 lakh cr and gold-jewellery loans 66.1% to ₹3.74 lakh cr; infrastructure lending grew only 5.9% ([RBI](https://www.rbi.org.in/Scripts/BS_PressReleaseDisplay.aspx?prid=63940)).
- *Second order:* this figure comes from RBI itself. Combined with last week's 41.8% growth in bank credit to NBFCs and the 83% growth in bank gold loans ([Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/latest/corporate/story/bt-explainer-gold-loans-surge-83-to-rs5-77-lakh-crore-what-happens-if-gold-prices-fall-15-564951-2026-11-01)), gold lending is now the fastest-growing retail pocket in both banks and NBFCs. Fast-growing pockets are the ones RBI's supervisors look at first.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: its Q2 gold AUM growth, due with listed-debt results by 14 Nov, will likely be read against the 66.1% sector figure rather than the 15.4% overall number.

**3. SEBI.** The Settlement of Administrative and Civil Proceedings Regulations, 2026 came into force on 7 Nov, with a 90-day window for proceedings pending at commencement ([SEBI PR 70/2026](https://www.sebi.gov.in/media-and-notifications/press-releases/nov-2026/commencement-of-sebi-settlement-of-administrative-and-civil-proceedings-regulations-2026_105680.html)). Digital KYC for non-resident individual clients applies from 3 Dec ([SEBI](https://www.sebi.gov.in/legal/circulars/nov-2026/review-of-know-your-client-process-for-individual-person-resident-outside-india-clients_105603.html)). The 5 Nov circulars on accredited investors, MF net settlement and currency-derivative limits, and the 2 Nov REIT/InvIT circulars, don't touch NBFC lending ([SEBI circulars](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebiweb/home/HomeAction.do?doListing=yes&sid=1&ssid=7&smid=0)).
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: none for lending. The NRI KYC change could make demat onboarding easier for non-resident holders of listed NCDs from December.

**4. Licensing: MobiKwik gets an NBFC licence.** RBI issued a Certificate of Registration to MobiKwik Financial Services, which plans to start lending in 3–6 months through co-lending ([NSE filing](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/corporate/MOBIKWIK_06112026141941_Intimation_about_granting_of_NBFC_License.pdf)).
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: another regulated lender aimed at Tier 2 and 3 small-ticket borrowers, and one that is looking for co-lending partners.

**5. Still pending.** The revolving-credit draft (6 Aug) and Interest Rate Directions draft (12 Aug) are not final ([RBI drafts](https://rbi.org.in/Scripts/DraftNotificationsGuildelines.aspx)). NBFCs are already swapping redraw facilities for term loans: Bajaj Finance's no-redraw variants started on 1 Nov, and Shriram's Umesh Revankar puts NBFC revolving credit at about ₹2 lakh cr ([ET](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/industry/banking/finance/nbfcs-redesign-lending-models-ahead-of-revolving-credit-curbs/articleshow/135512499.cms)). IRDAI's proposed commission caps are still a draft; HFCs told ET they would offset lost insurance income with higher fees and rates, and an ICRA study cited by ET put insurance distribution income at 8% of NBFC PAT in FY26 ([ET](https://economictimes.indiatimes.com/markets/stocks/news/nbfcs-may-raise-loan-rates-fees-to-offset-insurance-income-cap/articleshow/135667186.cms)). SEBI's Credit Risk-o-Meter goes live on 21 Nov ([SEBI](https://www.sebi.gov.in/legal/circulars/oct-2026/introduction-of-credit-risk-o-meter-as-an-additional-disclosure-mechanism-for-debt-securities_105081.html)).
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: the Risk-o-Meter applies to debt offer documents filed after 21 Nov, which would include later tranches under its ₹4,000 cr shelf. The IRDAI caps would matter only to the extent it earns insurance referral income.

## ⚠️ Asset Quality Deep Dive

**Gold loans: one more peer, and a hint of cure.** ETBFSI's 4 Nov piece adds detail. Shriram's gold-loan GS3 went to 3.12% from 2.57%. Bajaj Finance's gold Stage 2 rose ₹1,047 cr on short-tenure schemes, but ₹550 cr of that has already moved back to Stage 1, and Bajaj stopped the short-tenure product in August ([ETBFSI](https://bfsi.economictimes.indiatimes.com/articles/gold-loan-boom-raises-early-signs-of-borrower-stress-at-nbfcs/135478275)). That cure rate matters more than anything else published this week. If about half of a product-driven Stage 2 build can return to Stage 1 within a quarter, then much of the build is a servicing problem and not a credit problem.

**Collateral moved the right way.** Spot gold rose 5.2% on the week after a one-year low of $3,869.85 on 30 Oct ([ET/Reuters](https://m.economictimes.com/markets/commodities/news/gold-drops-as-strong-us-payrolls-boost-dollar-yields/amp_articleshow/135626183.cms)). In rupees the move was smaller: IBJA 999 ended Friday only 1.0% above the previous Friday, because the rupee also strengthened ([IBJA](https://ibjarates.com/)). AGLOC's 85% LTV ceiling went from ₹10,709/g (3 Nov) to ₹11,221/g (6 Nov) and back to ₹11,013/g for 7–9 Nov ([AGLOC](https://www.agloc.org/)). AGLOC is an industry body, so treat it as context, not an official rate. The rupee gold price is what sets LTV on Indian gold-loan books, and it barely moved on the week.

**Affordable housing and LAP: early buckets creeping up.** Aadhar Housing's Stage 2 rose about 29 bp to around 3.6%, its second straight quarter of deterioration, even as credit costs eased ([ETBFSI](https://bfsi.economictimes.indiatimes.com/articles/aadhar-housing-finance-sees-second-straight-quarter-of-asset-quality-deterioration/135624585)). Muthoot Capital Services (group company, not Muthoot Fincorp) saw GNPA rise to 4.81% from 3.94% in June ([NSE filing](https://nsearchives.nseindia.com/content/debt/WDM/MUTHOOTCAPS_05112026211829_Final.pdf)).

## 💡 Analyst's Take

Most of what happened this week came from outside India. One election result took 13% off oil, 22 bps off the 10-year and about half of the RBI hikes the swap market had priced. The surface story is a Diwali rally. Underneath it, the macro risk that drove NBFC funding costs up from August to October eased in five sessions. It can come back just as fast. Thursday's CPI is expected above 6% on food, and the RBI hike that the oil drop made less likely could still happen if that print is bad. The bond rally looks more like a window than a new regime.

On gold loans, the most useful data point this week was a footnote. Bajaj Finance moved ₹550 cr of a ₹1,047 cr gold Stage 2 build back to Stage 1 after it dropped the short-tenure product. That is the first public evidence that a rule-driven Stage 2 build can cure. Muthoot Finance's stock recovering 19% from its low says the market is willing to believe that, at least while gold is rising. The contrarian worry sits in the same data. RBI's own number says NBFC gold lending grew 66% in a year, banks grew theirs 83%, and dollar gold had its best week since August. A collateral class that grows that fast with that many new lenders tends to get supervisory attention after a good quarter, not after a bad one.

Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: Manappuram's call on 12 Nov lands two days before the 14 Nov listed-debt results deadline, so its Stage 2 and Stage 3 commentary is the comparison Muthoot Fincorp's Q2 numbers will meet. The 10 Nov holiday means the Tranche I listing date has to be reset.
