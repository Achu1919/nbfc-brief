### Other 18 Nov items (reported, not rule changes)

- SEBI chairman on margin trading. Business Standard reports that SEBI chairman Tuhin Kanta Pandey said on 18 Nov that SEBI continues to consider proposals to strengthen the margin trading facility, but has not taken a decision. He also said he does not see feasibility in extending market hours. This is a statement to the press. It is not a decision and not a circular, and it imposes nothing.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: none stated in the sources checked.
Sources: Business Standard, 18 Nov 16:44 IST, https://www.business-standard.com/markets/news/sebi-considering-proposals-to-strengthen-margin-trading-facility-chairman-126111800596_1.html

- Gold-loan LTV calculation for bullet-repayment loans. Business Standard, quoting SBI Securities' Sunny Agarwal, describes a revised LTV calculation for bullet-repayment loans. The total amount due at maturity is taken into account, and renewal is permitted only after accrued interest is paid. The 18 Nov instrument check above found no new RBI instrument, and the sources checked do not name the instrument this calculation comes from. It is an analyst's description, not a rule stated here.
Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: unclear. Muthoot Fincorp is a gold-loan NBFC, but the sources checked do not say whether this calculation applies to its loans.
Sources: Business Standard, 18 Nov 19:46 IST, https://www.business-standard.com/industry/news/gold-price-slide-drags-muthoot-finance-manappuram-shares-down-nearly-4-126111801259_1.html
