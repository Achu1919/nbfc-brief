### Gold slide hits gold-loan stocks: Muthoot Finance −3.97%, Manappuram Finance −4.59%
<!-- story: id=gold-slide-gold-loan-stocks-2026-11 -->
<!-- tags: companies=muthoot-finance,manappuram; topics=gold-price,gold-loan,markets,asset-quality; category=gold -->
Gold fell for a third straight session on 18 November. MCX December gold futures stood at ₹1,31,207 per 10 g, down ₹3,016 (−2.25%), which PTI described as the lowest level in over a year. Comex December gold futures fell $91.30 (−2.36%) to $3,772.90 an ounce. PTI and Business Standard attribute the move to a stronger US dollar, at a 19-month high, and expectations of further Federal Reserve rate hikes, ahead of the FOMC minutes.

Gold-loan stocks fell with it on the NSE close. Muthoot Finance, a listed peer and a separate company from Muthoot Fincorp, fell 3.97% to ₹2,640.00. Manappuram Finance fell 4.59% to ₹283.00. CNBC-TV18 reported that Manappuram had fallen 12.9% over five sessions and Muthoot Finance 10.5% over four.

Business Standard said the fall "raised concerns over collateral values, asset quality and the impact of tighter loan-to-value regulations". It reports that Muthoot Finance's Stage 3 assets rose more than twofold to ₹7,267 crore and its Stage 2 assets rose to ₹23,467 crore, citing the post-results analyst call. Those are Muthoot Finance figures. They are not Muthoot Fincorp's.

Manappuram Finance's management said the following to CNBC-TV18. Chairman and managing director VP Nandakumar said the company's loan-to-value is below 65%, so auctions are expected to be limited to 2% of the book. He said only about 1% of Stage 2 assets are expected to move to Stage 3, and Stage 3 is expected to fall to 1% as customers adapt to revised repayment norms. Stage 2 may stay at 5% to 8% before declining gradually. These are the company's expectations, not reported results. Manappuram's Stage 2 gold-loan assets rose to about 5% of its gold-loan portfolio in the September quarter, from about 0.5% in the quarter before.

On the rule side, Business Standard quotes SBI Securities' Sunny Agarwal describing a revised LTV calculation for bullet-repayment loans: the total amount due at maturity counts, and renewal is allowed only after accrued interest is paid. That is an analyst's description, not a new instrument; the rules check is in the Rules file.

Sector scale, per CNBC-TV18 citing CRIF High Mark: India's gold-loan market is ₹23.3 lakh crore, growing more than 60% over the past year. Q2 FY27 fresh gold-loan originations were ₹8.15 lakh crore by value, up 27.2% year on year, while the number of accounts originated fell 4.1% to 369.4 lakh. NBFCs' share of originations was 31.4%, up about 4 percentage points quarter on quarter.

Possible impact on Muthoot Fincorp: a lower gold price lowers the per-gram value of pledged collateral, so loans written close to an LTV ceiling have a thinner cushion. Muthoot Fincorp is a gold-loan NBFC. Its newest asset-quality figure in the sources checked is Stage III at 1.07%, from its Q2 filing of 14 Nov, which came before this fall. The Stage 2 and Stage 3 build described above is at Muthoot Finance, a listed peer.
